The briefing is generated via a structured five-phase research protocol executed against live web sources and Salesforce CRM. A strict 24-hour publication window is established at the start of each run. A minimum of 45 web searches and direct URL fetches are then executed across Tier-1 sources (TechCrunch, VentureBeat, Crunchbase News, Hacker News, Product Hunt, a16z, Axios) plus one targeted search per each of NVIDIA's 23 Inception verticals, casting a wide raw candidate pool. Each candidate article is then deep-read to extract funding stage, total raised, investors, founding team signals, traction metrics, and market timing — with any company exceeding $200M total raised dropped immediately. Three sequential Salesforce SOQL queries are run against the nvcrm-dev org for every surviving candidate: an Account lookup (checking AccountTags__c), an Inception Connect Profile lookup (checking Membership_Status__c), and an Opportunity count query — each capable of triggering immediate disqualification.

Surviving candidates are scored on a 13-point rubric across investor quality, founder signal, market timing, traction, and NVIDIA relevance. A vertical-diversity constraint (max 2 per vertical, target 7+ distinct verticals) is applied as a tiebreaker to ensure the final 10 represent broad coverage of NVIDIA's strategic focus areas. Each entry in the final output includes a company description, the news hook, investor syndicate, NVIDIA technology relevance mapped to specific products (BioNeMo, NIM, Jetson Orin, Omniverse, etc.), and Inception membership status. The briefing is saved as a dated Markdown file and is designed to be sent directly as an internal email to the NVIDIA Inception team.
