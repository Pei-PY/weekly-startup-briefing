The briefing is generated via a structured five-phase research protocol executed against live web sources, LinkedIn indexed discovery queries, optional authenticated LinkedIn connector/API enrichment when available, and a duplicate check against every earlier briefing. A strict 24-hour publication window is established at the start of each run. A minimum of 61 web searches and direct URL fetches are then executed across Tier-1 sources (TechCrunch, VentureBeat, Crunchbase News, Hacker News, Product Hunt, a16z, Axios), LinkedIn public post searches, plus one targeted search per each of the 23 tracked verticals, casting a wide raw candidate pool. Each candidate article or post is then deep-read to extract funding stage, total raised, investors, founding team signals, traction metrics, and market timing — with any company exceeding $200M total raised dropped immediately. Every surviving candidate is checked against all earlier briefings by normalized company name, parenthetical alias, and website domain; a company that has already been featured is disqualified, so each company appears in the series once.

Surviving candidates are scored on a 13-point rubric across investor quality, founder signal, market timing, traction, and technology relevance. A vertical-diversity constraint (max 2 per vertical, target 7+ distinct verticals) is applied as a tiebreaker to ensure the final 10 represent broad coverage of the tracked verticals. Each entry in the final output includes a company description, the news hook, investor syndicate, and technology relevance mapped to specific GPU products and SDKs. The briefing is saved as a dated Markdown file and is designed to be sent directly as an internal email.
