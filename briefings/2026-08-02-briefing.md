### 1. Sentient.io

**Vertical:** AI Developer Tools | **Total Raised to Date:** ~$7M | **Stage:** Series B1 | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**What we do:** Sentient.io runs an AI-as-a-Service platform that lets developers discover and embed pre-trained and data-driven AI models — speech, text, vision, and translation — through APIs, without provisioning their own compute or storage. Its models are tuned for Asian languages, including automatic speech recognition and transcription for Southeast Asian markets. Singapore HQ, founded 2017 by Christopher Yeo.

**Today's interesting [article](https://internationalfinance.com/technology/singapore-based-ai-startup-sentient-secures-funding-expansion/):** Sunday applied-AI review: Sentient.io's Real Tech Fund–led Series B1 positions it as one of the few SEA companies operating a horizontal AI-microservices marketplace rather than a single vertical model — the closest regional analog to a "model API layer" for Asian languages. As sovereign-AI programs (Singtel RE:AI, Malaysia, Indonesia) stand up in-region inference capacity through H2 2026, a language-tuned API aggregator is exactly the kind of application layer that rides on top of that compute.

**Investors:** Real Tech Fund (lead, Series B1), DG Ventures, Real Tech Holdings, among others

## Interesting Trends

1. **Sovereign-language AI is SEA's clearest GPU-native wedge** — Two of today's three highest-ranked companies (Sentient.io, Prosa.ai) are built on Asian- and Bahasa-Indonesia-tuned speech/NLP stacks — Riva/NeMo-shaped workloads that foreign frontier models under-serve. As Singtel RE:AI and other in-region sovereign-AI factories bring NVIDIA inference capacity online through H2 2026, language-tuned application layers are the natural demand layer riding on top, and the companies owning local-language data and models are best positioned to capture it.

2. **"AI securing AI" arrives as a fundable category in SEA** — cloudsineAI's multi-million-dollar public-sector contract to secure up to 100 GenAI applications shows that as agentic AI moves into production, the new attack surface (prompt injection, tool-call abuse, data leakage) is creating a distinct GenAI-security market. This is a Morpheus/NeMo-Guardrails-shaped workload — full-traffic inspection and runtime agent monitoring — and it grows in lockstep with every enterprise LLM deployment NVIDIA's ecosystem enables.

3. **Fintech's defensible asset is shifting from the app to the model** — Three of today's ten are fintechs (Bluesheets, Goline, GIMO), and each is explicitly moving compute-heavy intelligence to the center: IRIS document extraction across 75+ languages, FiinGroup-fed capital-markets data-AI, and AI-driven EWA underwriting at multi-million-worker scale. The distribution is commoditizing; the RAPIDS/Morpheus/NIM-accelerated risk, extraction, and analytics engines are becoming the moat — the layer where NVIDIA financial-services tooling (bunq-style ~100× risk training, 5× pipelines) is most relevant.
