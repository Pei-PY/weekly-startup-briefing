### 1. Racks Central

**Vertical:** Supercomputing / Global Public Sector | **Total Raised to Date:** $1B (strategic investment tranche) | **Stage:** Growth | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**What we do:** Racks Central is building the ASEAN AI-Ready Data Center Corridor — a network of hyperscale GPU data centers connecting Singapore, Johor (Malaysia), and Jakarta (Indonesia). The first ASEAN data center company explicitly designed for AI inference and training at hyperscale. Currently operational at 12MW in Singapore, with 510MW under development in Johor. Founded 2023.

**Today's interesting [article](https://www.dealstreetasia.com/stories/racks-central-strategic-investment/):** Racks Central secured a $1B strategic investment from CAF II (China-ASEAN Investment Fund) and ESR Group — the largest single investment in AI data center infrastructure in Southeast Asia. The Johor campus alone (510MW planned) represents enough compute capacity for a national AI program. At full capacity, it would be one of the top-5 AI compute facilities in Asia. The Singapore-Malaysia-Indonesia corridor positions ASEAN as a serious contender for global AI workload migration away from US data centers amid ongoing geopolitical realignment.

**Investors:** CAF II / China-ASEAN Investment Fund (lead), ESR Group

**NVIDIA Relevancy:** AI-ready data centers in Southeast Asia are the physical infrastructure on which every NIM, TensorRT-LLM, and GPU compute workload in the region runs. Racks Central's 510MW Johor campus is a natural candidate for NVIDIA's partner data center program — the AI compute infrastructure partnership (DGX SuperPOD, NVLink-based cluster deployments) is the most direct engagement path.
