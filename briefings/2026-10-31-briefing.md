### 1. Standard Physics

**Vertical:** Architecture, Engineering, Construction | **Total Raised to Date:** ~$2.3M | **Stage:** Seed | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**What we do:** Standard Physics builds a physics-informed AI tool that simulates and optimises building airflow, HVAC and energy systems, replacing slow computational-fluid-dynamics studies with fast surrogate models for engineers and building operators. Based in Singapore and backed by Y Combinator; the company previously operated under the name Inviscid AI.

**Today's interesting [article](https://www.vcbacked.co/company/standard-physics):** On October 29, 2026 Standard Physics closed a seed round led by Square Peg Capital (Tracxn and VCBacked list the round; amount undisclosed), lifting total funding to about $2.3M alongside Y Combinator's earlier backing, to commercialise its building-physics simulation for tropical data-centre and commercial-building markets.

**Investors:** Square Peg Capital (lead), Y Combinator

**NVIDIA Relevancy:** Physics-informed surrogate models for airflow and thermal behaviour could align directly with Modulus (PhysicsNeMo) and Omniverse for building digital twins; cuDF and Warp may be relevant to accelerating CFD pre-processing; TensorRT could apply to serving fast design-iteration inference.

**NVIDIA Case Study:** **Siemens** — couples physics simulation with Omniverse digital twins to cut HVAC and plant design cycles, the workflow Standard Physics compresses with AI surrogates ([case study](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/industries/architecture-engineering-construction/) · via [NVIDIA AEC page](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/industries/architecture-engineering-construction/))

### 2. Amplicity

**Vertical:** Energy (CleanTech / Oil & Gas) | **Total Raised to Date:** ~$1M | **Stage:** Seed | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟠 Maybe

**What we do:** Amplicity lets data centres and industrial sites use spare capacity in backup batteries they already own — UPS systems above the operator's reserve threshold — for peak shaving and wholesale-market revenue, with local controllers that enforce reserve limits even offline. Singapore-headquartered, founded by CEO Stéphane Leyo (ex-ENGIE Factory founder-in-residence) and COO Gabriel Schiano; it estimates 160 MWh of UPS capacity in Singapore data centres and 740 MWh across APAC.

**Today's interesting [article](https://e27.co/amplicity-raises-us1m-to-put-singapores-sleeping-data-centre-batteries-to-work-20261029):** On October 29, 2026 Amplicity announced a $1.01M seed backed by Loyal VC, citing more than 20 prospective sites in talks across Asia-Pacific and a 2027 Japan entry; its modelling claims a S$500,000 data-centre battery could yield about S$256,000 a year.

**Investors:** Loyal VC, ENGIE Factory

**NVIDIA Relevancy:** Battery-dispatch optimisation and market forecasting could potentially align with cuOpt and RAPIDS; the data-centre customer base makes Amplicity adjacent to AI infrastructure operators, but its own compute footprint is small and edge-controller based.

## Interesting Trends

1. **Data-centre adjacency is the new Singapore climate-tech pitch** — Standard Physics (cooling physics) and Amplicity (UPS batteries) both sell into the data-centre build-out rather than compete with it, a pattern that gives small startups large, well-funded customers.
