## Interesting Trends

1. **Philippines proptech AI is an underserved opportunity for NVIDIA Inception** — Lhoopa's $6M round addresses a 5.5 million home deficit using AI matching — but is currently low-compute. The opportunity: as the Philippines government digitizes property registries and national ID databases, AI platforms like Lhoopa will need GPU-accelerated geospatial and recommendation modeling to process citywide housing supply at real time. NVIDIA's RAPIDS library is a natural fit for this workload at scale.

2. **SuperAI Singapore 2026 closes with a capital formation signal** — The two-day event drew $2.3M in prize money for the Genesis Startup Competition, but more importantly brought together 1,500 AI companies in a pre-deal environment. Watch for Singapore and Malaysia-based AI startup announcements over the next 2–3 weeks as term sheets signed at SuperAI convert to public announcements.

3. **Malaysia is emerging as a dual play — chip design AND enterprise AI** — GreatAsic (chip design, Penang/Selangor) and Respond.io (customer AI, Kuala Lumpur, announcing next week) represent two distinct Malaysia AI bets emerging simultaneously. The National Semiconductor Strategy is pulling serious talent and capital into the design layer, while the Klang Valley startup ecosystem is producing enterprise SaaS companies at Series B scale. Malaysia's AI ecosystem is bifurcating productively.
