### 2. Walled AI

**Vertical:** AI Developer Tools | **Total Raised to Date:** undisclosed | **Stage:** Seed (Iterative W25) | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**What we do:** WalledAI is sovereign AI governance infrastructure that sits between employees/agents and any LLM (GPT, Claude, Gemini, Llama, Mistral, Copilot). It masks sensitive data before prompts reach a model, blocks prompt injections, validates outputs against ground truth, and logs every interaction for audit — with runtime guardrails (Redact, Protect, Correct) and sub-30ms latency. Singapore-built and IMDA-listed; it also publishes the open **WalledEval** LLM-safety test toolkit. Iterative Winter 2025 batch.

**Today's interesting [article](https://www.iterative.vc/post/meet-iteratives-winter-2025-batch):** Wednesday guardrails read: as SEA enterprises and regulators (Singapore's IMDA/AI Verify) push toward agentic AI in production, the "governance layer" is becoming a required purchase, not a nice-to-have. WalledAI's sub-30ms runtime firewall and open WalledEval benchmark position it as the region's pure-play on the exact problem NVIDIA productized as NeMo Guardrails — a rare infra-native SEA startup rather than another vertical app.

**Investors:** Iterative (Winter 2025 batch); other investors not disclosed

**NVIDIA Relevancy:** WalledAI's runtime firewall maps directly to NeMo Guardrails (topical/safety/security rails) and NeMo Retriever for ground-truth output validation; low-latency inline moderation maps to NIM microservices and TensorRT-LLM for its guardrail models; PII/prompt-injection classifiers align with Morpheus streaming pipelines; WalledEval-style red-teaming aligns with NeMo Evaluator. 3. **Clinically- and production-validated beats newly-funded for NVIDIA fit** — The strongest alignment today isn't the biggest raise: iota medtech (SingHealth-validated stroke triage) and ListenField (production models across 7 crops / 5 countries, running on Google Cloud) both have real deployed GPU-native workloads despite undisclosed funding.
