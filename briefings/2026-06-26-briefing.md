### 6. SpeQtral

**Vertical:** Supercomputing / Quantum (quantum-secure comms) | **Total Raised to Date:** ~$13.9M | **Stage:** Series A / growth | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**What we do:** SpeQtral (a CQT/NUS spinout) develops quantum-secure communications — quantum key distribution (QKD) across both terrestrial fibre and satellite links to protect networks against classical and future quantum attacks. Its product line includes SpooQies entangled-photon sources, the SQUINT ground station, and the Backend Quantum Unit; commercialization partners include SES, Thales Alenia Space, and Hispasat. Led by CEO Chune Yang Lum, HQ at Fusionopolis, Singapore.

**Today's interesting [article](https://speqtralquantum.com/newsroom/speqtre-the-entanglement-based-quantum-comms-demonstrator-satellite-is-now-on-orbit):** SpeQtral's SpeQtre CubeSat — an entanglement-based quantum-comms demonstrator carrying a space-qualified entangled-photon-pair source — reached sun-synchronous orbit on SpaceX Transporter-15 (Nov 28, 2025), with quantum experiments expected in early 2026 via ground stations at CQT (NUS) and RAL Space (UK). It is one of the few SEA deep-tech companies with hardware physically demonstrating space-based QKD — a milestone that de-risks the path to a commercial satellite-QKD service.

**Investors:** Airbus Ventures (early investor), plus institutional backers (Singapore's OSTIn supported; UK National Quantum Technologies Programme)
