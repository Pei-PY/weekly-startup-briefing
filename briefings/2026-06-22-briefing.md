### 2. Video Rebirth

**Vertical:** Media & Entertainment | **Total Raised to Date:** $80M | **Stage:** Series A | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**What we do:** Video Rebirth builds what it calls the world's first industrial-grade AI video generation engine — the Bach series — targeting commercial and enterprise content pipelines in entertainment, mobility, and marketing. Founded by Dr. Wei Liu (ex-Tencent AI Lab), the Singapore-headquartered company has pioneered Dual DiT architecture for prompt adherence and Physics Native Attention (PNA) for video consistency, with native 30fps output. Founded 2023.

**Today's interesting [article](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/video-rebirth-secures-80-million-to-build-the-worlds-first-industrial-grade-ai-engine-302716670.html):** Video Rebirth's $80M total round — an initial $50M plus a $30M extension — drew strategic investors AMD Ventures and Hyundai alongside Asian VCs, validating the company's cross-industry positioning. The AMD Ventures participation is the most notable signal: it places Video Rebirth inside AMD's GPU ecosystem while competing directly for the compute-intensive video generation market that NVIDIA's H100/B200 clusters currently dominate.

**Investors:** AMD Ventures (strategic), Hyundai (strategic), Asian VC firms (names undisclosed)

**NVIDIA Relevancy:** Industrial-grade video generation at the Bach series scale requires substantial GPU compute — H100/B200 clusters are the natural infrastructure for training and serving video diffusion models at 30fps native output. TensorRT-LLM and Triton Inference Server are directly relevant to Video Rebirth's inference pipeline, even as AMD is a strategic investor. RTX-accelerated rendering may be relevant for the real-time preview and editing workflows in their enterprise product line.

### 3. Polybee

**Vertical:** AgTech | **Total Raised to Date:** $4.3M | **Stage:** Seed | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**What we do:** Polybee deploys fleets of self-recharging autonomous micro-drones as "physical AI agents" in greenhouses and open-field agriculture — forecasting yields, detecting crop stress, and performing autonomous pollination using patented controlled-airflow technology. The company's subscription model delivers advanced agri-robotics without upfront capital. Headquartered in Singapore, with commercial operations in Australia, the US, and the UK. Founded 2019 by Siddharth Jadhav.

**Today's interesting [article](https://agfundernews.com/polybee-raises-4-3m-to-automate-yield-forecasting-and-pollination-with-physical-ai-agents):** Polybee raised $4.3M in seed funding led by Paspalis Capital and elev8 VC, with SEEDS Capital and Blue River Technology founder Jorge Heraud (acquired by John Deere for $305M) as a strategic angel. The Heraud participation is the standout signal — the founder of the company that defined AI-powered precision agriculture validating Polybee's physical AI approach is a meaningful endorsement of the technical direction.

**Investors:** Paspalis Capital (lead), elev8 VC (co-lead), SEEDS Capital, Jorge Heraud (angel)

**NVIDIA Relevancy:** Polybee's autonomous drone fleets require onboard edge AI for real-time crop analysis and navigation — a direct fit for NVIDIA Jetson Orin, which is the standard compute module for autonomous robots and drones in precision agriculture. Isaac ROS and Isaac Sim are relevant to Polybee's drone fleet orchestration and simulation pipeline. As the company scales from 4,000 acres to larger deployments, the compute requirements across the drone fleet create a natural Jetson footprint.

**Investors:** Vertex Ventures SEA & India (lead), Ehsan Kapital, Gobi Partners

**NVIDIA Relevancy:** GreatAsic designs custom ASICs for data center and Edge AI markets that could complement or compete with NVIDIA's GPU solutions depending on deployment context. ## Interesting Trends

*Three macro signals emerging from today's news:*

1. **Malaysia is emerging as SEA's chip design contender** — GreatAsic and the broader semiconductor talent density in Penang signal that Malaysia is actively pursuing front-end chip design, not just backend assembly and test. With government backing through MIDA and Arm IP access secured, Malaysia may be 3–5 years from producing its first domestically designed AI SoC at commercial scale — a market development directly relevant to NVIDIA's Edge AI ecosystem.

2. **Enterprise AI is proving revenue in SEA at scale** — Amity's $100M ARR at Series D and Respond.io's $35M ARR at 169% growth disprove the narrative that SEA enterprises won't pay for AI software. Both companies reached these numbers in markets historically resistant to SaaS pricing, suggesting that conversational and agentic AI is the vertical where SEA enterprises are willing to pay first — ahead of infrastructure AI or developer tools.

3. **Physical AI agents are finding product-market fit in agriculture** — Polybee's commercial traction across Australia, the US, and the UK — not just SEA — with drone-based AI pollination and yield forecasting signals that the physical AI category is moving beyond robotics labs into subscription deployments with measurable ROI. The Jorge Heraud angel investment (Blue River Technology, acquired by John Deere) validates the path: precision agriculture is where autonomous AI systems generate immediate, bankable returns.
