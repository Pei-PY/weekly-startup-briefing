### 1. Whale

**Vertical:** Smart Spaces | **Total Raised to Date:** ~$100M (Series C) | **Stage:** Series C | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**What we do:** Whale builds an enterprise AI Operating System (AIOS) that turns data from cameras, sensors, and audio devices into automation for physical operations, powered by a proprietary Business World Model (BWM) that interprets physical-world signals the way LLMs process text. Flagship products SpaceSight (video/sensor analytics of physical locations) and Echo (frontline sales-conversation scoring) serve 1,600+ enterprise customers across 45+ countries in retail, automotive, manufacturing, F&B, and financial services. Singapore HQ, founded by ex-Meta data scientist Jerry Ye; the platform manages 600,000+ edge AI nodes globally.

For NVIDIA, a 600,000-node edge-AI fleet analyzing camera/sensor/audio streams across 45 countries is one of the region's densest video-analytics deployments, and its expansion into MENA and Europe scales that edge footprint further.

**Investors:** CMB International (AI fund), SMBC Asia Rising Fund (co-leads), Krungsri Finnovate, Singtel Innov8, Hyundai Motor Group, Charisma Partners
