### 1. Drylab AI

**Vertical:** Healthcare & Life Sciences | **Total Raised to Date:** ~$3M | **Stage:** Seed | **Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**What we do:** Drylab builds autonomous AI research agents for pharma and biotech labs: the agents define strategy, select from 800+ scientific tools and databases, run computational workflows end to end, interpret results and troubleshoot failures. Founded in 2025 by a Vietnamese team — John Phan (CEO, ex-ByteDance Seed research), Huy Phan (CTO, ex-Landing AI) and Nhi Nguyen (Chief Science Officer) — headquartered in Singapore with a San Francisco presence; commercial launch was February 2026.

**Investors:** Vertex Ventures Southeast Asia & India (lead), PatSnap

**Relevancy:** Agentic scientific workflows could align with BioNeMo for the molecular and protein tools the agents orchestrate, NIM and NeMo Agent Toolkit for self-hosted agent serving inside pharma firewalls, and Clara Parabricks where genomics pipelines are in scope; Drylab publicly builds on third-party LLM APIs today.

### 2. Parallel Loop

**Vertical:** IT Services / Data Science / Mar-Legal-HR Tech | **Total Raised to Date:** ~$6.6M | **Stage:** Seed | **Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**What we do:** Parallel Loop positions itself as an "AI operating partner" for mid-market and large enterprises, pairing a platform (AI Stack, deployable in the client's cloud or on-premises) with forward-deployed engineers who identify use cases, integrate AI into existing systems and build custom workflows. Incorporated in Singapore and the US with teams in Singapore, Bengaluru, Bangkok, London and Dubai; founded by former Qoala executives Prateek Jogani and Yujun Chean, with Havells, Tokio Marine and Licious as early customers.

**Today's interesting [article](https://e27.co/parallel-loop-nets-us6-6m-to-sell-enterprises-the-one-thing-ai-models-lack-context-20261014/):** On October 14, 2026 Parallel Loop launched publicly with a $6.6M seed led by 3one4 Capital, joined by Picus Capital, Flourish Ventures and Economic Development Innovations Singapore, to scale across APAC, Europe and the Middle East.

**Investors:** 3one4 Capital (lead), Picus Capital, Flourish Ventures, Economic Development Innovations Singapore (EDIS)

**Relevancy:** An on-premises enterprise AI stack could align with NVAIE, NIM and NeMo for private model serving and fine-tuning, TensorRT-LLM and Dynamo for inference efficiency, and NeMo Retriever for the enterprise-context layer the company sells; no current vendor relationship is disclosed.

## Interesting Trends

1. **"Forward-deployed engineering" is Singapore's enterprise-AI export** — OneByZero and Parallel Loop both raised on a services-plus-platform model that embeds engineers inside clients, a response to the 57% of SEA firms that cannot scale AI pilots; it also means inference runs in client clouds and data centres, not the startup's.
2. **Vietnamese founders are building from Singapore** — Drylab joins AI Hay and others in a pattern where Vietnamese technical teams incorporate in Singapore for capital access while keeping research talent at home.
3. **Capital concentration hit an extreme** — three Singapore-registered, China-origin companies (Kling, Manus, Supabase's local entity) took roughly 98% of October's $3.68B; the qualifying early-stage SEA deal flow underneath was fewer than fifteen rounds.
