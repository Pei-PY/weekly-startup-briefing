## Interesting Trends

1. **The ASEAN AI compute corridor is now a real geopolitical asset** — Racks Central's $1B investment (backed by China-ASEAN Investment Fund) places ASEAN's AI infrastructure strategy in the context of US-China tech competition. A Singapore-Malaysia-Indonesia data center corridor funded by Asian capital provides a third-party compute option for AI companies unwilling to choose between AWS/GCP/Azure (US-aligned) and Alibaba/ByteDance (China-aligned). NVIDIA must be the hardware layer — losing the GPU supply contract to AMD or Huawei Ascend in this corridor would be strategically significant.

2. **Malaysia-Indonesia bilateral tech corridor is the most significant ecosystem development in ASEAN in 2026** — Digital Penang × KUMPUL creates the first formalized Malaysia-Indonesia startup ecosystem bridge. Malaysia contributes semiconductor/hardware manufacturing expertise and Penang's startup ecosystem; Indonesia contributes market scale (270M consumers) and Batam's manufacturing base. NVIDIA Inception could use this corridor as a co-recruitment channel: bilateral Inception application workshops and a "ASEAN AI hardware track" would capture both countries' strongest hardware AI startups.
