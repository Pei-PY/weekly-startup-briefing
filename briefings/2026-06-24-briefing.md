### 1. Perceptra

**Vertical:** Healthcare & Life Sciences | **Total Raised to Date:** Undisclosed (seed) | **Stage:** Seed | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**What we do:** Perceptra builds Inspectra, an AI platform that reads chest X-rays at radiologist level in real time, flagging up to eight abnormalities including lung cancer, tuberculosis, and other lung diseases. Co-developed with Siriraj Hospital (Mahidol University), the system is certified by the Royal College of Radiologists of Thailand, the Thai FDA, and Singapore's HSA, and is on the Thai Innovation List. **Today's interesting [article](https://www.nationthailand.com/health-wellness/40055280):** Perceptra's AI chest X-ray screening is scaling through Thailand's public-health system on a phased national rollout — 167 hospitals in 2025, 445 hospitals in 2026, and a planned 887 by 2027 — deployed under the country's "30-Baht Gold Card" universal coverage programme to address a national radiologist shortage. The AstraZeneca A.Catalyst Network / Lung Ambition Alliance partnership positions Perceptra as Thailand's reference AI-imaging vendor for equitable early lung-cancer detection.

**Investors:** Individual/angel investors (2022 seed, led by an individual investor); strategic partners AstraZeneca (Thailand), Siriraj Hospital

### 2. optoML

**Vertical:** AI Developer Tools (Semiconductors) | **Total Raised to Date:** ~$1.8M | **Stage:** Pre-Series A | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**What we do:** optoML is a fabless semiconductor startup building an Analog-in-Memory-Compute (AiMC) AI SoC with on-chip optical interconnects, designed to scale from edge to hyperscale data-center AI inference. Its patented in-memory-compute architecture claims up to 50× higher energy efficiency than traditional digital accelerators, at lower latency and silicon area. Singapore HQ with an R&D center in Bengaluru.

**Today's interesting [article](https://startupnews.fyi/2026/02/24/chip-startup-optoml-raises-1-8-million-from-bluehill-vc-a99-completes-12-nm-tapeout/):** optoML raised a $1.8M pre-Series A led by Bluehill.VC and A99, announced alongside completion of a **12nm TSMC tapeout** realizing its core AiMC architecture and SoC integration path. It has signed an MoU with Kaynes Semicon for assembly and test once wafers return from TSMC — a rare SEA fabless-AI-silicon milestone. Founder Saravana Maruthamuthu brings 17 years across Intel, Qualcomm, Continental, and Isar Aerospace.

**Investors:** Bluehill.VC (lead), A99 (co-lead)

**NVIDIA Relevancy:** Custom AI-silicon design intersects the NVIDIA ecosystem at the software layer. CUDA-X-accelerated EDA flows (circuit simulation, computational lithography via cuLitho) could potentially compress optoML's verification and tapeout cycles; CUDA and TensorRT-LLM compatibility decisions ahead of production determine whether the resulting AiMC SoCs align with the dominant AI software stack; and Nsight profiling tools may be relevant to benchmarking inference-efficiency claims against a common baseline.

3. **Agentic AI is arriving as vertical process automation, not general assistants** — SPUN (visa processing) and Vbee (enterprise callbots) show SEA agents attacking narrow, high-friction workflows with measurable outcomes (99% visa approval; 70–90% audio-cost reduction).
