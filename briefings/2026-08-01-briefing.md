### 1. Mindverse AI

**Vertical:** AI Developer Tools | **Total Raised to Date:** ~$39M | **Stage:** Seed | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**What we do:** Mindverse AI builds a proprietary **Large Personalization Model (LPM)** and the **MindOS** platform — giving AI systems long-term memory, complex reasoning, and per-user personalization. Its products span MindOS Studio (AI-native websites with personalized chat/agents for enterprises) and Mebot (a personal "second brain"). Founded January 2022 in Singapore by ex-Meta executives and AI researchers.

**Today's interesting [article](https://technode.global/2024/04/12/singapores-ai-firm-mindverse-secures-5m-in-seed-funding-led-by-square-peg/):** Weekend deep-tech read: Mindverse's thesis — that AI moves from generalized language models to *personalized* experiences requiring their own memory and reasoning layer — puts it in the same "memory-as-the-missing-layer" race now attracting SEA VC attention. A pre-trained Large Personalization Model with long-term memory is a fundamentally GPU-native workload, making Mindverse the most compute-intensive company in today's briefing and Singapore's clearest personalization-model bet heading into H2 2026.

**Investors:** Square Peg (lead), among others

**NVIDIA Relevancy:** NeMo Framework (Megatron-Core, LoRA) could align with pretraining/fine-tuning the Large Personalization Model; NeMo Retriever and cuVS are relevant to the long-term-memory retrieval layer; NIM microservices and TensorRT-LLM map to serving MindOS agents at multi-tenant scale; NeMo Guardrails to safe personalized outputs; Merlin/Transformers4Rec could apply to preference modeling over user profiles.

### 3. GeneStory

**Vertical:** Healthcare & Life Sciences | **Total Raised to Date:** Undisclosed (Vingroup / VIC Partners-backed) | **Stage:** Seed | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**What we do:** GeneStory is a Vingroup spin-out (Hanoi, Vietnam) providing AI-driven **disease-risk prediction and pharmacogenomic analysis** built on a large Vietnamese genomic dataset (the "1,000 Vietnamese Genomes"/VN1K project, 42M+ variants). Its **VinGenChip** is the first biochip designed on Vietnamese population genomics, analyzing tens of thousands of variants for drug-response and disease-risk screening. A pharmacogenomics package covers 100+ drugs. Won VietChallenge 2022.

**Today's interesting [article](https://vovworld.vn/en-US/society/vietchallenge-winner-aims-to-make-genomic-services-affordable-to-all-vietnamese-1162337.vov):** Weekend genomics read: GeneStory's wedge is population specificity — disease-risk and drug-response models trained on Vietnamese genomes rather than Western reference panels, which mispredict for Asian populations. Population-specific variant calling and polygenic risk scoring are among the most GPU-acceleratable workloads in life sciences, and a Vingroup-backed team sitting on a proprietary 42M-variant Vietnamese dataset is SEA's clearest sovereign-genomics AI opportunity.

**Investors:** Vingroup, VIC Partners

## Interesting Trends

1. **Accelerator cohorts are SEA's dominant early-stage AI funnel — and they arrive with GPU credits attached** — Nugen (Antler AI Disrupt), Moneta (Iterative demo-day), and Valtor (Wavemaker Impact venture-build) all reached this briefing via structured accelerator pipelines rather than open-market rounds. Antler's AI Disrupt bundling ~$1M capital *with GPU compute perks* is the clearest signal: SEA accelerators are now provisioning compute as a first-class asset, which puts NVIDIA's ecosystem in front of these companies at pre-seed — exactly Inception's origination window.

2. **Sovereign local-language and local-population data is the region's most defensible AI moat** — Looloo (Thai OCR/ASR/GenAI), GeneStory (Vietnamese population genomics), and Mindverse (personalization models) all build on proprietary local data that foreign APIs serve poorly. These are the most GPU-native companies in today's set — Thai speech models, Vietnamese variant calling, and personalization pretraining are all workloads NVIDIA's Riva/Parabricks/NeMo stack directly accelerates — making sovereign-data startups the highest-conviction NVIDIA fits across SEA.

3. **The "undisclosed early-stage" long tail is where Inception leverage is highest, not lowest** — Half of today's companies (Moneta, GeneStory, iCarus, FirstChip, Looloo) carry undisclosed or grant-only funding, and several (iCarus, FirstChip) have light AI/compute today. Rather than screening these out, the pattern to watch is trajectory: FirstChip's tape-out toolchain choice, iCarus's edge-inference path, and Moneta's build-vs-API decision are each a fork toward or away from the NVIDIA stack — decisions best influenced now, at grant stage, when a single GPU-credit or Inception touch has maximum impact.
