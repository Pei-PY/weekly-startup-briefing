## Interesting Trends

1. **Singapore is becoming the sovereign AI model hub of SEA** — Sapiens AI's Agnes AI (5M users in 12 months, trained entirely in Singapore) reflects a deliberate national agenda: Singapore wants its own AI stack, not dependency on OpenAI or Google. With Budget 2026's $1B Startup SG Equity expansion and National AI Strategy 2.0, Singapore is betting that sovereign AI models are a strategic infrastructure play — not just an academic exercise.

2. **Physical AI is the highest-conviction early-stage bet in SEA** — Both Polybee (agricultural drones) and Muun AI (machine telemetry intelligence) represent a shift from pure software AI toward AI embedded in physical systems. The Jorge Heraud angel investment in Polybee is the best validation signal: the founder of Blue River Technology (acquired by John Deere) is betting on physical AI in agriculture again, this time in Singapore.

3. **SEA's EV two-wheeler market is fragmenting between Vietnam-domestic and Thailand-regional** — Dat Bike (Vietnam-only, $51M total) and SLEEK EV (Thailand-focused, $8.5M) represent two distinct strategies: hyper-local ownership in a large domestic market vs. regional expansion from a smaller base. Both are approaching AI software as a differentiation layer rather than a core product — signaling that EV hardware commoditization in SEA is accelerating faster than anticipated.
