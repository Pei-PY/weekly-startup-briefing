### 1. Hivebotics

**Vertical:** Robotics | **Total Raised to Date:** ~$6M | **Stage:** Series A | **Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**What we do:** Hivebotics builds Abluo, a mobile robot with an articulated arm that cleans entire commercial restrooms — toilets, urinals, sinks and floors — using contactless high-pressure steam, run by its HiveIntelligence software layer for planning, monitoring and verifying cleaning. Founded in 2023 out of the National University of Singapore's Innovation and Design Programme; Abluo launched commercially in 2024 and has logged about 10,000 cleaning hours across 20 sites.

**Today's interesting [article](https://technode.global/2026/09/04/singapores-hivebotics-raises-6m-led-by-vertex-to-scale-restroom-cleaning-robots/):** On September 4, 2026 Hivebotics closed a $6M Series A led by Vertex Ventures Southeast Asia & India, with Fareast Land Development (Farglory Group) and smart-restroom maker Rigel joining, to move Abluo into volume production and expand its distributor network. Vertex framed the bet on labour supply: restroom cleaning is non-discretionary demand with a tightening workforce.

**Investors:** Vertex Ventures Southeast Asia & India (lead), Fareast Land Development, Rigel

**Relevancy:** Manipulation and perception for a restroom-cleaning arm could align with Isaac ROS (cuMotion, nvblox, cuVSLAM) and JetPack for onboard inference; Isaac Sim and Isaac Lab may be relevant to training and validating arm policies across restroom layouts; TensorRT could apply to real-time surface and fixture detection.

**Case Study:** **Brain Corp** — runs autonomous commercial floor-cleaning robots on GPU-accelerated edge software, the closest facilities-management analog to Abluo's restroom cleaning ([case study](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/industries/robotics/) · via [robotics industry page](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/industries/robotics/))

### 2. nanoSkunkWorkX

**Vertical:** Supercomputing / Quantum | **Total Raised to Date:** ~$2M (plus undisclosed Gobi round) | **Stage:** Seed | **Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**What we do:** nanoSkunkWorkX (nSWX) develops thin-film interface materials that improve how heat, current and signals move across material boundaries in advanced semiconductor packaging — the thermal bottleneck of dense AI chip packages — with a second line in catalysts for green hydrogen. Founded in 2019 in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia by CEO Iqbal Shamsul and CTO Dr. Amani Salim, a former NASA scientist.

**Today's interesting [article](https://technode.global/2026/09/02/nanoskunkworkx-2m-tin-men-ai-chip-packaging/):** On September 2, 2026 nSWX closed a $2M seed led by Tin Men Capital — the B2B-focused fund's first-ever Malaysian investment — building on earlier backing from Gobi Dana Impak and Kumpulan Modal Perdana. Funds go to semiconductor partner qualification, scaling the interface-engineering process platform and validation work.

**Investors:** Tin Men Capital (lead), Gobi Partners (Dana Impak), Kumpulan Modal Perdana

**Relevancy:** Materials R&D of this kind could align with Modulus for physics-informed thermal simulation and CUDA-X HPC libraries for interface modelling; cuQuantum may be relevant to electronic-structure calculations behind new interface chemistries. The company's output targets the packaging supply chain for AI accelerators broadly rather than any single vendor.

### 3. Superbench

## Interesting Trends

1. **Vertex is building a physical-AI portfolio in Singapore** — Hivebotics follows Vertex's other 2026 robotics and hardware bets, and the investment thesis is explicitly about labour scarcity in non-discretionary services, which favours robots that work alongside, rather than replace, human cleaners.
2. **Singapore VCs are finally crossing the Causeway for deep tech** — Tin Men Capital's first Malaysian cheque went to a packaging-materials company, a sign that Malaysia's semiconductor back-end strength is now being read as a startup opportunity rather than only a multinational one.
3. **Payment flexibility is the wedge for SEA consumer platforms** — Laters.com (pay-later flights), Superbench (conversation-to-payment) and Lumio's appliance financing all compete on how customers pay rather than what they buy, keeping compute needs light and fintech-adjacent.
