### 1. fileAI

**Vertical:** IT Services / Data Science / Mar-Legal-HR Tech | **Total Raised to Date:** ~$20M+ (latest strategic round undisclosed) | **Stage:** Series A | **Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**What we do:** fileAI (formerly bluesheets) builds an enterprise AI data-preparation platform that extracts, structures and validates information from complex unstructured documents — invoices, bank statements, contracts — and routes it into governed, audit-ready workflows (fileForge) and a new unstructured-data mapping tool (fileScout). Founded in Singapore by Christian Schneider and Clare Leighton; customers are banks, insurers and finance teams across Asia and Japan.

The capital funds a Japan team build-out and a financial-services product push; amount undisclosed.

**Investors:** SMBC Asia Rising Fund, Singtel Innov8, Illuminate Financial, Antler Elevate, Insignia Ventures Partners, Heinemann Group, Krungsri Finnovate

**Relevancy:** Document-understanding pipelines of this kind could align with NeMo Retriever and NIM microservices for multimodal extraction and RAG over enterprise documents; TensorRT-LLM and Triton may be relevant to serving validation models at bank-scale throughput; NVAIE could support on-premises deployment demanded by Japanese and Singaporean financial institutions.

### 2. Living Roots

**Vertical:** AgTech | **Total Raised to Date:** ~$0.5M (seed undisclosed; prior bridge led by Epic Angels) | **Stage:** Seed | **Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**What we do:** Living Roots designs biological crop inputs ("crop protocols") that replace chemical fertiliser and burn-based crop-residue disposal, using an AI platform called Hypha to adapt formulations to crop type, soil condition and growth stage. Founded in 2021 in Chiang Mai, Thailand by computer scientists Abhi Agarwal (CEO) and Avika Narula; a trial with a Thai dairy producer reported a 50% yield improvement in corn silage.

**Today's interesting [article](https://www.agnavigator.com/Article/2026/08/26/living-roots-targets-full-stack-biologicals-with-ai-led-rd-after-latest-funding/):** On August 25, 2026 Living Roots announced an undisclosed seed round to build a full-stack biologicals business with AI-led R&D, and two weeks later (September 7) it was one of 16 projects selected for Google DeepMind's inaugural AI for the Planet accelerator in Asia-Pacific — the only Thai company on the list.

**Investors:** Epic Angels (bridge lead), Southeast Asian angel investors, seed investors undisclosed

**Relevancy:** Formulation-design models over soil, microbiome and crop data could align with RAPIDS and cuML for tabular/omics modelling; BioNeMo may be relevant if the company moves into microbial strain discovery; TAO Toolkit and DeepStream could apply to field-imagery crop-health monitoring that feeds protocol adjustments.

## Interesting Trends

1. **Japanese corporate venture money is the marginal buyer of SEA enterprise AI** — SMBC, Singtel Innov8, Leave a Nest and (the following week) Ricoh all wrote cheques into Singapore and Malaysia software and deep tech in a fortnight when regional VC was quiet. Strategic LPs want distribution into Japan, which pushes startups toward on-premises, data-resident deployments.
2. **Sovereign digital-trust rails are becoming a startup surface** — PILA's national-chain role and the TradeTrust letter-of-credit pilot show Vietnam and Singapore treating identity and traceability as infrastructure startups can operate, creating demand for secure networking and anomaly analytics rather than model training.
3. **Malaysia is seeding silicon ambition through program access, not capital** — the Arm Flexible Access awards to Alphaswift and Oppstar signal a policy bet that domestic chip design, even for avionics, starts with IP access; the compute stacks that result will be Arm-first, with GPU acceleration a prospective layer.
