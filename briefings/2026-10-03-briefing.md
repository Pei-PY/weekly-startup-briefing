### 1. Nexstrom

**Vertical:** Supercomputing / Quantum | **Total Raised to Date:** ~$15M (incl. $3M non-dilutive) | **Stage:** Seed | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**What we do:** Nexstrom develops wafer-scale two-dimensional semiconductor materials — single-crystal 2D films grown on 12-inch (300mm) production wafers — for next-generation AI, data-centre and HPC chips, combining proprietary chemical-vapour-deposition hardware with process technology. Founded in 2024 in Singapore and incubated by Temasek-backed Xora Innovation; Stanford's Philip Wong advises the board.

**Today's interesting [article](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/22/singapores-nexstrom-wants-to-bring-2d-semiconductors-to-chip-fabs/):** On September 22, 2026 Nexstrom announced a $12M seed led by Xora Innovation with Foothill Ventures and SEEDS (SG Growth Capital), bringing total funding to $15M, to commercialise its 2D platform and deliver 300mm single-crystal wafers on production-ready tools.

**Investors:** Xora Innovation (lead), Foothill Ventures, SEEDS Capital (SG Growth Capital)

**NVIDIA Relevancy:** Nexstrom's materials target the transistor-scaling roadmap of all advanced-logic makers, including AI accelerators; its own R&D could align with Modulus and CUDA-X HPC libraries for growth-process simulation, and cuQuantum may be relevant to electronic-structure modelling of 2D materials. No current NVIDIA relationship is disclosed.

### 2. TacniQ.ai

**Vertical:** Robotics | **Total Raised to Date:** ~$1.5M (of a targeted $3M pre-seed) | **Stage:** Pre-Seed | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**What we do:** TacniQ.ai builds tactile-AI foundation models that let robots and machines interpret touch, backed by proprietary sensor nodes and a dataset of more than 5,000 hours of tactile interactions; it works with Synaptics to turn research into commercial tactile applications. Based in Singapore and California and led by co-founder and CEO Aashish Mehta, formerly of NI's ASEAN applications engineering team.

**Today's interesting [article](https://technode.global/2026/09/22/singapores-tacniq-ai-raises-1-5m-from-in-group-holdings-in-pre-seed-round/):** On September 22, 2026 TacniQ.ai secured $1.5M from lead investor In Group Holdings as the first close of a $3M pre-seed, funding tactile foundation-model development, engineering hires and first commercial deployments.

**Investors:** In Group Holdings (lead)

**NVIDIA Relevancy:** Tactile foundation models could align with Isaac Lab and Isaac Sim for sim-to-real manipulation training; Isaac GR00T and Cosmos may be relevant to multimodal policy learning that fuses touch with vision; TensorRT and JetPack could apply to deploying tactile inference on robot edge compute.

**NVIDIA Case Study:** **Boston Dynamics** — trains manipulation and locomotion policies in Isaac Sim before sim-to-real transfer, a workflow TacniQ's touch models would plug into ([case study](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/industries/robotics/) · via [NVIDIA robotics page](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/industries/robotics/))

### 3. iPiD

**Vertical:** FinTech / FSI | **Total Raised to Date:** ~$24.6M | **Stage:** Series A | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**What we do:** iPiD (International Payments Identity) is a Singapore payment-intelligence network that verifies beneficiary names and account details before funds move (iPiD Validate) and helps banks meet Confirmation-of-Payee rules (iPiD Node), across 100+ markets. Founded in late 2021 by ex-Swift executives Damien Dugauquier (CEO), Eddie Haddad and Geertjan van Bochove; Citi uses the technology inside Citi Verify.

**Today's interesting [article](https://technode.global/2026/09/25/singapore-ipid-16m-series-a-foundation-capital/):** On September 24, 2026 iPiD announced a $16M Series A led by Foundation Capital with Citi and HSBC as strategic investors and QED, Monk's Hill, Quona and 1982 Ventures returning, to expand into the US and Europe and build verification for US payment rails and stablecoins.

**Investors:** Foundation Capital (lead), Citi, HSBC, QED Investors, Monk's Hill Ventures, Quona Capital, 1982 Ventures

**NVIDIA Relevancy:** Real-time payee verification and fraud-risk scoring across bank networks could align with Morpheus for streaming anomaly detection and RAPIDS for graph and entity-resolution analytics; Triton may be relevant to serving low-latency risk models inside bank data centres.

**NVIDIA Case Study:** **American Express** — accelerates real-time transaction fraud models on NVIDIA software for millisecond decisions, an analog to iPiD's pre-payment verification ([case study](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/industries/financial-services/) · via [NVIDIA financial services page](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/industries/financial-services/))

## Interesting Trends

1. **Singapore is producing silicon-layer startups, not just AI apps** — Nexstrom (2D wafers) and September's nanoSkunkWorkX (packaging interfaces) attack the two physical bottlenecks of AI compute, both incubated by Temasek-linked or Singapore-based deep-tech investors.
2. **Global banks are investing directly in SEA payment intelligence** — Citi and HSBC on iPiD's cap table (after SBI into dtcpay) signal that verification and compliance tooling is where banks want equity exposure, which makes on-premises, low-latency inference the deployment norm.
3. **Corporate venture builders are the seed engine in Vietnam and Malaysia** — Tasco's Foundry (Bonbon), 1337 Ventures (Cashku) and Aravest (August Energy) show corporates manufacturing their own pipeline where independent seed funds are scarce.
