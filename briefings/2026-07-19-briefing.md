### 1. Gowajee

**Vertical:** Media & Entertainment | **Total Raised to Date:** Undisclosed (accelerator-backed) | **Stage:** Seed / Accelerator | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**What we do:** Gowajee is a Bangkok-based Thai-language voice-AI company spun out of Chulalongkorn University's Computer Engineering department (team led by Dr. Ekapol Chuangsuwanich). It builds automatic speech recognition (ASR), text-to-speech (TTS), and automatic speaker verification tuned natively for Thai, plus a QA Agentic AI voice-agent product. It compiled a ~5,000-hour Thai speech corpus and reports ~9% word-error-rate versus ~15% for generic engines, with enterprise clients including Generali, IT One, and SCG.

**Today's interesting [article](https://wowsglobal.com/resources/blogs-insights/company-spotlight-gowajee-the-future-of-thai-language-ai-voice-technology/):** Sunday SEA sovereign-language-AI read: Gowajee is the clearest example this week of a university speech lab converting a proprietary local-language dataset into an enterprise voice moat. Thai ASR/TTS accuracy is a data problem before it is a model problem, and Gowajee's 5,000-hour corpus plus Chula pedigree gives it defensibility that generic multilingual APIs cannot match — exactly the GPU-native, low-latency inference profile NVIDIA's speech stack is built for.

**Investors:** Krungsri Finnovate (Finno Efra Accelerator), EfraStructure; additional backers undisclosed

**NVIDIA Relevancy:** Riva is directly relevant to Thai ASR/TTS and speaker verification pipelines; NeMo (and NeMo Curator for the 5,000-hour corpus) may align with training and fine-tuning custom Thai acoustic and voice models; TensorRT-LLM and NIM could serve the QA Agentic AI voice agent under real-time latency SLAs; NeMo Guardrails may apply to enterprise voice-agent safety in regulated clients like Generali.

**NVIDIA Case Study:** **Amdocs** — built telecom voice agents and domain LLMs on NVIDIA Riva and NIM, measuring 4–6× faster inference and ~80% lower latency serving conversational AI — the closest NVIDIA reference for a real-time local-language voice-agent platform like Gowajee ([case study](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/amdocs-accelerates-generative-ai-performance-and-lowers-costs-with-nvidia-nim/) · via [NVIDIA media & entertainment page](https://www.nvidia.com/en-sg/industries/media-and-entertainment/))

**Vertical:** Robotics | **Total Raised to Date:** Undisclosed (acquired by Nanoveu, May 2026) | **Stage:** Acquired / Deep-tech spinout | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**What we do:** Spinoff Robotics is a Singapore deep-tech spinout from the Singapore University of Technology and Design (SUTD), founded 2021 by Assoc. Prof. Foong Shaohui with SUTD doctoral researchers. It builds two commercially deployed platforms: **ALICE**, a tethered drone engineered for high payloads and jamming/spoofing resistance, and **METRON**, a camera-based 3D-measurement system with AI deviation detection for millimetre-level infrastructure inspection. Tier-1 Singapore customers include Gardens by the Bay, the Land Transport Authority, and the Home Team Science & Technology Agency (HTX).

**Investors:** Nanoveu Limited (ASX: NVU) — acquirer; earlier backing via SUTD deep-tech pathways

## Interesting Trends

1. **Indonesian financial-services rails are consolidating around bank-strategic capital** — Three of today's ten (Yup, OY!, Bang Jamin) are Indonesian fintech/insurtech, and the money increasingly comes from banks and bank-linked funds: MUFG's MUIP Garuda Fund and Bank Danamon into OY!, BNI Ventures and BRI Ventures into Bang Jamin, Telkomsel's TMI into Feedloop. As these platforms scale to millions of transactions and 100K+ policies per day, credit scoring, fraud, and AML become the RAPIDS/Morpheus workloads where NVIDIA's FSI stack is most differentiated — and the bank backers are the channel that already knows it.

2. **SEA robotics risks consolidating on non-NVIDIA edge silicon** — Spinoff Robotics' acquisition by ASX-listed Nanoveu completes a full-stack drone play built around Nanoveu's *own* ECS-DoT edge-AI chip, not Jetson. This is the week's clearest competitive-watch signal: as SEA physical-AI companies get absorbed by hardware parents pushing proprietary accelerators, NVIDIA's leverage shifts from silicon to the software layer (Isaac ROS, Metropolis, Isaac Sim synthetic data) — where perception and simulation portability still matter.

3. **University-lab language and voice moats are the region's most GPU-native early bets** — Gowajee (Chulalongkorn) turned a proprietary 5,000-hour Thai speech corpus into an enterprise voice-agent business with a measurable accuracy edge (~9% vs ~15% WER), while Feedloop pushes compliance-first, on-prem agentic AI. Both are exactly the GPU-native, sovereign-AI profiles — Riva/NeMo training, NIM on-prem serving — that NVIDIA Inception is designed to catch early, ahead of the FinTech and platform names already visible through banking channels.
