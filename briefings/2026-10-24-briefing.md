### 1. Genie Health

**Vertical:** Healthcare & Life Sciences | **Total Raised to Date:** ~$3M | **Stage:** Seed | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**What we do:** Genie Health builds a personal health-intelligence platform: a free consumer app (Genie) that makes sense of a person's scattered health records, labs and wearable data, and SmartCare, an agentic-AI product for clinics. Based in Singapore and co-founded by former PropertyGuru CEO Hari V. Krishnan, Maya Hari and Sandeep Suvarna.

**Today's interesting [article](https://e27.co/ex-propertyguru-ceos-genie-health-raises-us3m-to-make-sense-of-your-scattered-health-data-20261021):** On October 21, 2026 Genie Health closed a $3M seed led by the Nadathur Family Office (founded by Infosys co-founder N.S. Raghavan) with Temasek-backed founders' studio Liminal participating; Anand Nadathur joins the board and the funds go to AI capability, clinic adoption of SmartCare and Singapore hiring.

**Investors:** Nadathur Family Office (lead), Liminal

**NVIDIA Relevancy:** Agentic clinical assistants over patient records could align with NIM and NeMo Retriever for privacy-preserving retrieval, NeMo Guardrails for clinical safety constraints, and MONAI if imaging reports are brought into the record graph; the company has not disclosed its model stack.

**NVIDIA Case Study:** **Abridge** — generates clinical documentation from patient encounters with GPU-accelerated language models, an analog for Genie's SmartCare clinic workflow ([case study](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/industries/healthcare-life-sciences/) · via [NVIDIA healthcare page](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/industries/healthcare-life-sciences/))

### 2. Blue Whale Energy

**Vertical:** Energy (CleanTech / Oil & Gas) | **Total Raised to Date:** ~$6M | **Stage:** Seed | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟠 Maybe

**What we do:** Blue Whale Energy develops distributed battery-storage systems for commercial and industrial rooftops in Singapore and aggregates them into the country's first battery-based virtual power plant, earning revenue from grid services and peak shaving. Singapore-based and led by CEO Gabriel Lim, who previously built and sold sales-automation startup Saleswhale.

**Today's interesting [article](https://dealroom.co/news/163726-blue-whale-energy-raises-4m-seed-to-build-singapore-grid-batteries/):** On October 19, 2026 Blue Whale Energy announced a $4M seed with Wavemaker Partners, SG Growth Capital (SEEDS) and ADB Ventures as new investors and Analog Ventures, Monk's Hill Ventures and UntroD Capital returning, a year after a $2M Forge Ventures-led round, to fund projects already underway and expand its Singapore storage footprint.

**Investors:** Wavemaker Partners, SG Growth Capital (SEEDS), ADB Ventures, Forge Ventures, Analog Ventures, Monk's Hill Ventures, UntroD Capital, Yamada Shokai

**NVIDIA Relevancy:** Virtual-power-plant dispatch across many batteries could potentially align with cuOpt for optimisation and RAPIDS for price and load forecasting; Modulus may be relevant to battery degradation modelling. Current compute needs are modest and cloud-based.

## Interesting Trends

1. **Thai corporates are buying their startup ecosystem** — Betagro taking control of Freshket (after CVC's talks with Tofusan) shows conglomerates acquiring platforms rather than funding rounds, which shortens the independent life of Thai startups and moves their tech roadmaps in-house.
2. **Family offices are leading Singapore seeds** — the Nadathur Family Office (Genie Health) and, the following week, Loyal VC and family offices (Amplicity, Ailytics) stepped in where institutional seed funds were quiet.
3. **Distributed energy is Singapore's quiet climate-tech cluster** — Blue Whale's VPP, Amplicity's idle-battery monetisation and NEU's recycling plant point to a storage-centric cluster whose compute needs are optimisation rather than model training.
