### 1. Spacely AI

**Vertical:** Architecture, Engineering & Construction (AEC) | **Total Raised to Date:** ~$1M | **Stage:** Seed | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**What we do:** Spacely AI is a Thailand-based generative-AI platform that turns sketches, 3D models, and room photos into photorealistic renders in under 60 seconds — with AI virtual staging, image editing, relighting, and a 2D-to-3D automation engine that removes up to 80% of manual concept work. It plugs directly into SketchUp (Extension Warehouse) and other CAD tools, serving architecture and interior-design professionals. Bangkok HQ.

**Today's interesting [article](https://technode.global/2025/07/18/thailands-spacely-ai-secures-1m-seed-round-ed-by-proptech-farm-fund-iii/):** Spacely AI raised a US$1M seed led by PropTech Farm Fund III (following a pre-seed from SCB 10X), and the traction is what stands out for a design-tools company at this stage: 10× revenue growth in a year, 1,500+ architecture/interior firms served across 50+ countries, and over two million unique renders generated. That render volume is a recurring diffusion-inference workload — the exact GPU-native economics NVIDIA's media/rendering stack targets.

**Investors:** PropTech Farm Fund III (lead), SCB 10X (pre-seed), Wannaporn Phornprapha (P Landscape), Ted Poshakrishna Thirapatana (UTC Holdings), Mek Srunyu Stittri (ex-GitLab VP Engineering)

**NVIDIA Relevancy:** Spacely's render pipeline is diffusion-model inference at scale — TensorRT and Triton/Dynamo could potentially accelerate photorealistic image generation and cut per-render latency; NIM diffusion microservices may align with its SketchUp-integrated 2D-to-3D service; DALI and nvImageCodec are relevant to the image preprocessing pipeline; Omniverse/OpenUSD could apply to structured 3D-scene generation for CAD interoperability. Its render backend runs on Replicate (vendor-hosted GPUs) per the company site, so the stack is not NVIDIA-exclusive today.

**NVIDIA Case Study:** **Runway** — develops its top-rated Gen-4.5 generative video/image models entirely on NVIDIA GPUs across pre-training, post-training, and inference, including real-time HD generation with sub-100ms time-to-first-frame — the benchmark NVIDIA-stack analog for a diffusion-based render engine like Spacely's ([case study](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/leading-models-nvidia/) · via [NVIDIA media & entertainment page](https://www.nvidia.com/en-sg/industries/media-and-entertainment/), used as the generative-render match since the AEC page routes design-viz not generative-diffusion customers)

### 4. Entropica Labs

**Vertical:** Supercomputing / Quantum | **Total Raised to Date:** Undisclosed (Seed) | **Stage:** Seed | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**What we do:** Entropica Labs is a Singapore quantum-software company internationally recognized for quantum error correction (QEC), hardware-aware circuit compilation/transpilation, and circuit optimization — the software layer that makes fault-tolerant quantum computing practical across different qubit hardware. Its work spans fault-tolerant QEC architectures and hardware-specific optimization.

**Today's interesting [article](https://thequantuminsider.com/2026/01/12/yaqumo-inc-and-entropica-labs-sign-mou-witnessed-by-singapore-and-japan-governments/):** Entropica Labs signed an MOU with Japan's Yaqumo Inc. — witnessed by Singapore and Japanese government representatives — to co-develop hardware-aware transpilation and optimization technologies tailored specifically to cold-atom quantum computers. Government-witnessed cross-border QEC collaboration signals sovereign-scale investment in the quantum software stack, and QEC decoding is exactly the GPU-accelerated simulation workload NVIDIA's CUDA-Q / cuQuantum platform targets.

**Investors:** Undisclosed (seed-stage; prior backing from deep-tech quantum investors); Yaqumo Inc. (R&D collaboration partner via MOU)

**NVIDIA Relevancy:** Entropica's core workloads are textbook cuQuantum / CUDA-Q territory: cuQuantum (cuStateVec, cuTensorNet) could potentially accelerate the noisy circuit simulation needed to validate QEC codes; CUDA-Q and CUDA-QX (QEC library, BP+OSD decoders) may align with its fault-tolerant decoder development; CUDA-Q's hybrid model is relevant to hardware-aware transpilation for cold-atom systems. This is a software-only match — quantum hardware maps to the CUDA-Q / cuQuantum software layer.

**NVIDIA Case Study:** **QuEra, SEEQC & Infleqtion** — accelerated quantum error-correction research with NVIDIA CUDA-Q QEC and CUDA-QX, using the GPU-accelerated BP+OSD decoder for qLDPC codes (order-of-magnitude / ~30× decoder speedup) and generating trillions of noisy shots on GPU — the same QEC-decoding and hardware-aware simulation workload Entropica builds ([case study](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/accelerating-quantum-error-correction-research-with-nvidia-quantum/) · via [NVIDIA supercomputing page](https://www.nvidia.com/en-sg/industries/supercomputing/))

## Interesting Trends

1. **Generative AI is arriving in SEA as vertical media pipelines, not chatbots** — Three of today's ten (Spacely's render engine, Bythen's autonomous avatars, Aiko's real-time sports narratives) are generative-media companies stacking diffusion, digital-human, and real-time-inference workloads — the exact GPU-native pipelines NVIDIA's Audio2Face / RTX-rendering / Holoscan-for-Media stack targets. SEA's generative-AI wedge is turning out to be applied media production rather than foundation models.

2. **The compute-ecosystem battle is already visible at pre-seed** — Aiko enters with strategic AMD, Microsoft, and Verizon relationships at a US$1.5M pre-seed, meaning its real-time inference stack sits on AMD silicon and Azure before NVIDIA has engaged. For a generative-media company this young, the incumbency of a competing GPU ecosystem is a signal that NVIDIA ecosystem outreach has to happen at pre-seed, not Series A.

3. **Agentic AI is the connective tissue across unrelated SEA verticals** — Autonomous or semi-autonomous LLM agents show up in CX (Filum's 100K-conversation agent at 99% CSAT), healthcare (HD's Jib AI handling ~60% of interactions; Eluvo's roadmap), and avatars (Bythen's autonomous "brain"). The common infrastructure need — low-latency, token-efficient agent serving — is precisely the NIM / TensorRT-LLM / NeMo Guardrails value proposition (Amdocs: 4–6× faster inference, up to 60% fewer tokens).
