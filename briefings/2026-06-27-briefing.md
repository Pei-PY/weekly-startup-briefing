## Interesting Trends

1. **SEA's "physical AI" wave is arriving as embodied edge-autonomy, not just software** — Three of today's five 🟢 companies (ROLO's cooking robots, OMOWAY's self-balancing "two-wheeled robot," Nvsion's line-speed AOI) are hardware products whose defensibility lives in an on-device perception-decision-execution loop. That is precisely the Isaac / DriveWorks / Metropolis territory, and it puts SEA squarely in NVIDIA's robotics-and-edge lane rather than the API-wrapper lane — a shift from prior briefings dominated by software SaaS.

2. **Sovereign and strategic capital is underwriting SEA deep tech** — Khazanah's Cambrian Fund (Nvsion), Malaysia's VentureTECH (Delta Spike), Khazanah again via PolicyStreet, BHP Ventures + In-Q-Tel (Atomionics), and XPeng/BYD-linked funds (OMOWAY) show national and industrial strategics, not just VCs, funding the region's compute-heavy companies. For NVIDIA these are accounts where a government or industrial partner already has skin in the game — natural co-sell surfaces for sovereign-AI and manufacturing initiatives.

3. **The generative-content thesis is splitting gaming into GPU-native vs. GPU-light** — Onibi bets its entire design on generative models producing living, per-player worlds (an ACE/NeMo-native workload), while Lihuhu's $103.8M NCSoft exit was built on high-velocity hyper-casual publishing with a light compute footprint. Both are "gaming," but only one is a genuine NVIDIA-stack company — a useful filter as SEA gaming M&A accelerates.
