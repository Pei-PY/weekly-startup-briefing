### 2. PrismML

**Vertical:** AI Developer Tools | **Total Raised to Date:** Undisclosed | **Stage:** Seed | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Active user

**What we do:** PrismML makes language models compressed to run on a single GPU or mobile device using 1-bit quantization — storing each model weight as a single bit instead of 16 or 32 bits. Their Bonsai 8B model is 14x smaller and 8x faster than a standard 8B model.

**Today's interesting [article](https://prismml.com/):** Their Bonsai 1.7B model runs at 130 tokens per second on an iPhone 17 Pro Max, and the launch hit #1 on Hacker News with 418 upvotes — strong developer signal that small-model inference on commodity hardware is reaching a practical inflection point.

**Investors:** Khosla Ventures, Cerberus Capital, Caltech, Google

**NVIDIA Relevancy:** Bonsai's extreme model compression could potentially align with Jetson Orin-class edge hardware deployments.

## Interesting Trends

1. **Physical AI hardware is the new software infrastructure** — Anvil Robotics shipping "Legos for robots" at seven-figure ARR in 8 months signals that the physical AI hardware gap is real and fundable. As NVIDIA's GR00T program accelerates, the demand for modular, low-cost robot hardware will compound. Inception should be sourcing physical AI hardware companies the same way it sources software startups.

2. **Edge compression is unlocking Jetson's full potential** — PrismML's 1-bit Bonsai models (14x smaller, 8x faster) demonstrate that the LLM-on-edge dream is now an engineering reality. Google Gemma 4 also dropped this week (open weights, single GPU). Combined, this week marks a clear inflection: capable LLMs now run on Jetson-class hardware without compromise. Every robotics, industrial, and edge IoT Inception company benefits.

3. **Defense AI is moving from R&D to production contracts** — Edgerunner AI's WarClaw has SOCOM, Navy, and Lockheed Martin contracts at launch — no VC needed. The DoD's accelerated AI adoption post-2025 is creating a generation of defense-native AI startups that go straight to government contracts. DDIL edge AI = Jetson Orin. Inception should be tracking this vertical more aggressively.
