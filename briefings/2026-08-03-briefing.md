### 1. Allozymes

**Vertical:** Healthcare & Life Sciences | **Total Raised to Date:** ~$20M | **Stage:** Series A | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**What we do:** Allozymes is a Singapore biotech that pairs a proprietary microfluidic screening platform with AI/ML to discover and engineer novel enzymes for sustainable manufacturing (pharma, food, agriculture, cosmetics). Its ultra-high-throughput system screens over 20 million enzyme variants per day — an industry-leading rate the company claims delivers ~200× higher odds of finding a custom biocatalyst. Singapore HQ; spun out of the NUS/EF/SOSV deep-tech ecosystem.

**Today's interesting [article](https://www.finsmes.com/2022/05/allozymes-raises-15m-in-series-a-funding.html):** Allozymes' $15M Series A (Seventure Partners, Xora Innovation, NUS Technology Holdings, Thia Ventures, with SOSV and Entrepreneur First following on) is explicitly earmarked to build "the industry's most comprehensive enzyme data library" — a proprietary dataset that AI/ML models then use to predict and generate new biocatalysts. That data-flywheel framing is what makes Allozymes NVIDIA-relevant in Aug 2026: it is a Southeast Asian wet-lab company converting physical screening throughput into a GPU-trainable protein-design corpus, the exact pattern NVIDIA's BioNeMo ecosystem was built for.

**Investors:** Seventure Partners, Xora Innovation, NUS Technology Holdings, Thia Ventures, SOSV, Entrepreneur First

**NVIDIA Relevancy:** BioNeMo (protein language models, ESM-2/geometric models) could align with Allozymes' enzyme sequence-to-function modeling; NeMo may apply to training on its proprietary variant library; RAPIDS cuDF/cuML is relevant to screening-data analytics at 20M variants/day; Triton and TensorRT could serve inference for variant ranking. No public evidence of the current silicon vendor — modeling appears cloud-based.

**NVIDIA Case Study:** **Evozyne** — built the ProT-VAE generative protein framework on NVIDIA BioNeMo (ProtT5) plus a variational autoencoder, generating millions of candidate sequences in seconds and designing enzymes with function beyond anything in nature — the direct analog to Allozymes' AI-driven enzyme engineering ([case study](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/generative-ai-proteins-evozyne) · via [NVIDIA healthcare & life sciences page](https://www.nvidia.com/en-sg/industries/healthcare-life-sciences/))

### 2. VISAI

**Vertical:** Global Public Sector | **Total Raised to Date:** Undisclosed | **Stage:** Strategic-backed (SCB 10X / DEPA / VISTEC) | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**What we do:** VISAI AI builds a frontier Thai-language LLM stack for law, compliance, and governance. Its flagship **FourCorners** (สี่มุมเมือง) legal-research platform is grounded on a temporal legal knowledge graph of ~552K nodes spanning 3,800+ active Thai laws and 87,000+ Supreme Court decisions; a second product, **Vocalog**, handles compliance voice/transcription. Bangkok HQ; led by CEO Sarana Nutanong, who also serves as Dean of VISTEC's School of Information Science & Technology, tying the company to Thailand's national Thai-LLM research base.

**Today's interesting [article](https://visai.ai/):** VISAI's positioning as a "Legal Blue Ocean" play — a domain-specialized Thai LLM rather than a general chatbot — is the more defensible ASEAN sovereign-AI thesis heading into H2 2026. The FourCorners knowledge graph (552K nodes over 3,800 laws) is exactly the kind of structured, high-value corpus that a general frontier model cannot easily replicate, and its VISTEC lineage links it to WangChanGLM/Typhoon-class Thai model research — making VISAI one of the clearest NeMo/NIM sovereign-AI fits in this briefing.

**Investors:** SCB 10X, DEPA (Digital Economy Promotion Agency), VISTEC (research affiliation) — round size not disclosed

**NVIDIA Relevancy:** NeMo (Megatron-Core, Curator) could align with pretraining/fine-tuning a sovereign Thai legal LLM; NIM microservices are relevant to on-prem regulated-sector deployment where data residency matters; NeMo Retriever and cuVS map to the FourCorners knowledge-graph RAG layer; Riva could serve Vocalog's Thai ASR/transcription; NeMo Guardrails for compliance-grade output control.

## Interesting Trends

1. **Sovereign, national-language AI is the strongest early-stage NVIDIA thesis in SEA right now** — VISAI's frontier Thai legal LLM (552K-node knowledge graph, VISTEC lineage) and, prospectively, Anissa AI's native-Thai content model are both plays on the same demand SEA-LION crystallized: regions building their own NeMo-trained, NIM-served language foundations rather than renting foreign APIs. The wet-lab equivalent shows up in Allozymes, which is converting 20M-variant/day physical screening into a proprietary, GPU-trainable enzyme corpus — a "sovereign dataset" for BioNeMo-class modeling. Across all three, the moat is proprietary local data feeding an NVIDIA-native training stack.

2. **The physical-world edge (vision + physics-ML) dominates this cohort's real GPU workloads** — Konstruksi.AI (jobsite machine vision), Asia Mobiliti (IOroad road-defect CV + cuOpt routing), Lambdai Space (physics-aware radar imaging), and Chaptu (roadmap CV chick-health) are all Metropolis/DeepStream/Modulus-shaped rather than chatbot-shaped. This is the pattern NVIDIA Inception is best positioned to support in SEA: companies with live production imagery and sensor data whose acceleration gap is edge inference and physics-informed modeling, not just LLM serving.

3. **Accelerator and strategic-corporate backing — not disclosed rounds — is the dominant capital signal** — Half this cohort has undisclosed funding routed through Antler AI Disrupt (Lambdai, Konstruksi.AI), Iterative/AEON (3cat), ENGIE (GetSolar), SCB 10X/DEPA/VISTEC (VISAI), and Shark Tank TH (Agnos). For Inception origination this is a feature: these are pre-institutional companies where program support has maximum leverage, and where corporate strategics (ENGIE, AEON, SCBX) are already providing distribution — the ideal moment to align them to the NVIDIA software stack before their compute decisions harden.
