### 1. Bifrost AI

**Vertical:** Auto / AV Infra | **Total Raised to Date:** ~$13.7M | **Stage:** Series A | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**What we do:** Bifrost AI (Singapore, founded 2019) generates physically accurate synthetic datasets in 3D simulation to train and validate computer-vision models for autonomous vehicles, robotics, maritime, geospatial, and defense — without the cost and risk of collecting real-world data. Its engine produces 3D worlds at scale, simulating rare and dangerous edge cases (traffic accidents, illicit-vessel detection at sea, planetary landings) with perfect ground-truth labels.

**Today's interesting [article](https://feature-asia.com/features/10-to-watch/ai-infrastructure-founders-singapore-2025/):** Bifrost sits at the exact intersection NVIDIA is building toward with Cosmos and Omniverse — synthetic data as the fuel for physical AI. With Airbus Ventures, Peak XV (Surge), and Singapore defense-linked CAP Vista on the cap table, Bifrost is one of SEA's rare deep-tech infrastructure plays whose entire product is a GPU-native simulation-and-rendering workload. As real-world AV/robotics data becomes the binding constraint on model quality across the region, synthetic-data generation is the pick-and-shovel layer NVIDIA most wants healthy in ASEAN.

**Investors:** Carbide Ventures (lead), Airbus Ventures, Peak XV Partners (Surge), Wavemaker Partners, Hustle Fund, CAP Vista, MD One Ventures, Techstars

**NVIDIA Relevancy:** Bifrost's synthetic-data engine maps directly to Omniverse Replicator (domain randomization, ground-truth generation) and OpenUSD for scene assembly; physically based rendering pipelines align with CUDA/OptiX; downstream perception-model training and validation map to TAO Toolkit, DALI (data loading), and TensorRT for optimized inference benchmarking. For AV-specific scenario generation, DRIVE Sim/Replicator and Cosmos world-foundation-model workflows are the closest analogs to Bifrost's product.

**NVIDIA Case Study:** **NVIDIA DRIVE Sim + Omniverse Replicator** — NVIDIA's own AV team uses Omniverse Replicator on DRIVE Sim to bootstrap perception networks, fill real-world data gaps, and auto-label ground truth for rare/dangerous scenarios — the identical synthetic-data-for-perception workload Bifrost productizes for third parties ([case study](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/drive-sim-replicator-synthetic-data-generation/) · via [NVIDIA automotive page](https://www.nvidia.com/en-sg/industries/automotive/))

### 2. Qritive

**Vertical:** Healthcare & Life Sciences | **Total Raised to Date:** ~$7.5M | **Stage:** Series A | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**What we do:** Qritive (Singapore, founded 2017) builds **Pantheon**, a fully customizable digital-pathology platform with AI plugins that manage histopathology lab cases and assist pathologists in analyzing whole-slide images for cancer diagnosis. Its AI modules grade malignancies across breast, prostate, colon, immunohistochemistry, and blood smears, and Pantheon is registered as a Class A Medical Device with Singapore's HSA. The platform trains with a network of 150+ pathologists and has analyzed close to half a million images.

**Today's interesting [article](https://www.medicalstartups.org/country/Singapore/):** Digital pathology is one of the most GPU-hungry corners of clinical AI — whole-slide images are gigapixel-scale, and both training and inference are bottlenecked on image I/O and tiling. Qritive's regulatory clearance plus a GE Healthcare investor relationship makes it a credible ASEAN reference for AI-assisted cancer diagnosis, precisely the workload NVIDIA accelerates with MONAI and cuCIM. As Southeast Asia's pathologist shortage deepens, AI triage of slides is shifting from research to reimbursed clinical practice.

**Investors:** GE Healthcare, MassMutual Ventures, Pix Vine Capital

**NVIDIA Relevancy:** Whole-slide image analysis maps to MONAI (pathology training/inference pipelines, NVTX profiling) and cuCIM (GPU-accelerated gigapixel image loading and tiling); model serving in-lab maps to Triton and TensorRT; the broader clinical-imaging workflow aligns with Clara Deploy and Holoscan SDK for streaming inference. RAPIDS may accelerate the analytics over patient metadata paired with slide features.

**NVIDIA Case Study:** **Mayo Clinic** — is building AI-powered digital-pathology tools on MONAI, running whole-slide tumor-detection pipelines accelerated with cuCIM and RAPIDS for real-time analysis — the same gigapixel histopathology workload behind Qritive's Pantheon cancer-grading modules ([case study](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/whole-slide-image-analysis-in-real-time-with-monai-and-rapids/) · via [NVIDIA healthcare & life sciences page](https://www.nvidia.com/en-sg/industries/healthcare-life-sciences/))

## Interesting Trends

1. **Physical-AI and synthetic-data infrastructure is arriving in SEA at the pick-and-shovel layer** — Bifrost AI (synthetic data for AV/robotics/defense) and RIFFAI (satellite earth-observation ML) are both selling the GPU-native *inputs* to physical AI rather than end applications, backed by strategic investors (Airbus Ventures, CAP Vista). This is the layer NVIDIA most wants healthy in the region — Omniverse Replicator, Cosmos, and geospatial digital twins — because it compounds into every downstream robotics and AV workload.

2. **Local-language and local-context AI is the defensible moat for SEA software companies** — GENEXYZ (Bahasa metahumans), Hekate.ai (Vietnamese government chatbots), and Enfarm (crop-specific advisory) all win on data and context outsiders can't easily replicate. Each maps to a specific NVIDIA local-language/edge stack (Riva, NeMo, Audio2Face, TensorRT), making sovereign and vernacular AI the region's most reliable source of GPU-native early-stage companies.

3. **FinTech's next compute wave is fraud/AML and credit decisioning, not payments plumbing** — Three of today's ten are FSI (Tookitaki, Salary Hero, Higala), and the acceleration opportunity is uniform: the payments and EWA rails themselves are CPU-bound transactional software, but the fraud, AML, and credit-risk layers riding on top are exactly where RAPIDS, Morpheus, and cuGraph deliver the ~100× training and false-positive reductions (bunq, UOB) that translate into direct compliance-cost savings.
