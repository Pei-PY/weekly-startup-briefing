### 1. Eureka Robotics

**Vertical:** Robotics | **Total Raised to Date:** ~$15M | **Stage:** Series A | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**What we do:** Eureka Robotics builds AI-powered robotic software and a projector-free 3D camera for "High Accuracy – High Agility" (HA-HA) tasks — precision picking, object recognition, and inspection in factories and warehouses. Spun out of NTU Singapore (with research lineage to MIT and the University of Tokyo), the company has run 25M+ HA-HA operations across factories worldwide. Singapore HQ, founded 2018, now scaling into the U.S.

**Today's interesting [article](https://technode.global/2024/12/13/singapores-eureka-robotics-raises-10-5-in-series-a-funding-led-by-b-capital/):** Eureka's $10.5M Series A (led by B Capital, with Airbus Ventures joining) funds U.S. expansion of its physical-AI vision stack — and Airbus Ventures on the cap table signals aerospace-grade precision manufacturing as a target market. For NVIDIA, this is a textbook physical-AI account: a GPU-native 3D-perception + control-loop use case, at Series A, with no competing GPU-ecosystem lock-in disclosed — the highest-leverage robotics engagement stage.

**Investors:** B Capital (lead), Airbus Ventures, ATEQ, existing seed backers

**NVIDIA Relevancy:** Eureka's projector-free 3D reconstruction and bin-picking control loop map to Isaac ROS (cuVSLAM, nvblox, FoundationPose for 6-DoF pose estimation) and TensorRT for on-arm inference; Isaac Sim / Isaac Lab and Omniverse Replicator are relevant to synthetic training data and sim-to-real for new part geometries; cuMotion could apply to collision-free motion planning in cluttered picking scenes.

### 2. Katalon

**Vertical:** AI Developer Tools | **Total Raised to Date:** ~$27M | **Stage:** Series A | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**What we do:** Katalon builds an AI-augmented software test-automation platform used by 30,000+ teams across 80+ countries, spanning web, API, mobile, and desktop testing plus a TestOps orchestration layer. Its Katalon Scout / StudioAssist features apply generative AI to auto-generate, self-heal, and explain tests from natural language. Product operations centre in Vietnam (founded out of the KMS Technology group), with a global commercial footprint.

**Today's interesting [article](https://vir.com.vn/katalon-raises-27-million-in-series-a-funding-led-by-elephant-partners-85200.html):** Katalon's $27M Series A (led by Elephant Partners) makes it one of Vietnam's best-capitalised pure-software AI companies — and its move from a free tool to an AI-native testing platform is the wedge into the fast-growing "agentic QA" market. **Investors:** Elephant Partners (lead)

1. **Physical-AI vision is SEA's strongest robotics wedge — but the silicon is contested** — Two of today's ten (Eureka Robotics, Widya Robotics) are 3D/LiDAR-vision companies solving high-value industrial measurement and picking. The pattern for NVIDIA: SEA physical-AI companies are real and GPU-native by use case, but edge-silicon relationships form early — engagement at seed/Series A is decisive.

2. **Vietnam is quietly becoming SEA's applied-AI language lab** — Three Vietnam-built companies (Katalon, Revve AI, PangoCDP) span QA automation, banking voice agents, and CDP — and Revve's proprietary Vietnamese banking voice model (sub-1-second latency, trained on domain conversations) is the standout GPU-native workload. 3. **Cyber-AI splits into "compute-heavy detection" vs "workflow GRC"** — CYFIRMA (predictive external-threat management, continuous telemetry ingestion) is a Morpheus/RAPIDS-shaped account rated 🟢; Cyber Sierra (governance, compliance, embedded insurance) is workflow-and-data software rated 🟠.
