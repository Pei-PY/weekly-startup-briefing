### 1. Video Rebirth

**Vertical:** Media & Entertainment | **Total Raised to Date:** $80M | **Stage:** Series A | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**What we do:** Video Rebirth builds industrial-grade AI video generation (BACH engine) around two proprietary architectures — Dual Diffusion Transformer (DDiT) for directorial control and Physics-Native Attention (PNA) for character consistency built from bone structure and muscular dynamics — with 30fps native output. Founded by Dr. Wei Liu (ex-Tencent AI Lab distinguished scientist, IEEE Fellow). Singapore HQ.

**Today's interesting [article](https://technode.global/2026/03/18/singapores-video-rebirth-secures-80m-funding-to-build-ai-engine/):** August H2 follow-up: since the $80M close in March, BACH launched publicly on May 7 and — per Artificial Analysis' Video Arena, the largest blind AI-video benchmark — debuted at **#6 in the world**, level with Kling 3.0 Omni and Grok Video. **Investors:** AMD Ventures, Hyundai Motor Group (via ZER01NE), CJ Group (HIVEN / CJ ENM), Qiming Venture Partners, Actoz Soft

**NVIDIA Relevancy:** Video Rebirth runs a mixed accelerator fleet spanning both AMD and NVIDIA silicon (per launch PR; specific parts documented in the Acceleration column below) — so despite AMD Ventures' strategic investment, a substantial NVIDIA footprint is already in place across training and serving, making this a competitive-retention account rather than greenfield.
