### 1. ChemLex

**Vertical:** Healthcare & Life Sciences | **Total Raised to Date:** ~$45M (this round; cumulative undisclosed) | **Stage:** Growth | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**What we do:** ChemLex is an AI-for-science company that pairs generative chemistry models with a 24/7 autonomous "self-driving lab" to accelerate small-molecule and materials discovery for pharma. Founded 2022, it has grown in three-and-a-half years to serve 70+ customers worldwide — including six of the world's top ten pharmaceutical companies. It is establishing its global HQ and self-driving laboratory in Singapore.

**Today's interesting [article](https://technode.global/2025/12/08/ai-for-science-startup-chemlex-raises-45m-launching-self-driving-lab-for-drug-discovery-in-singapore/):** ChemLex closed a US$45M round led by Granite Asia and signed an MoU with Singapore's Experimental Drug Development Centre (EDDC) to co-accelerate next-gen small-molecule discovery. For NVIDIA this is the highest-value physical-AI-meets-life-sciences account in SEA this window: an autonomous wet-lab loop (generate → synthesize → assay → learn) is exactly the workload BioNeMo, Parabricks and Isaac lab-automation were built to serve, in a market analysts expect to grow from $3.6B (2024) to ~$50B by 2034.

**Investors:** Granite Asia (lead), with strategic backing tied to its Singapore EDB / EDDC expansion

**NVIDIA Relevancy:** ChemLex's generative-chemistry and virtual-screening pipeline maps directly to BioNeMo NIMs (MolMIM, GenMol, DiffDock for docking, and property predictors); its autonomous synthesis/assay loop maps to Isaac ROS / Isaac Sim for lab robotics and cuOpt for experiment scheduling; genomics/assay analytics could align with Parabricks and RAPIDS cuDF; and its molecular language models to NeMo and TensorRT-LLM for serving. All prospective fits are expressed in software terms.

**NVIDIA Case Study:** **Life-sciences leaders on BioNeMo (Chai Discovery, Basecamp Research, Boltz)** — these AI-drug-discovery teams connect BioNeMo, agentic AI and physical AI to run lab-in-the-loop virtual screening at scale, the direct analog to ChemLex's generative + self-driving-lab loop ([case study](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-bionemo-platform-adopted-by-life-sciences-leaders-to-accelerate-ai-driven-drug-discovery) · via [NVIDIA healthcare & life sciences page](https://www.nvidia.com/en-sg/industries/healthcare-life-sciences/))

### 2. ByteRover

**Vertical:** AI Developer Tools | **Total Raised to Date:** Undisclosed (pre-seed) | **Stage:** Pre-Seed | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**What we do:** ByteRover builds an agent-native memory layer for AI coding agents — a self-improving, LLM-curated hierarchical context store that plugs into Cursor, Windsurf, Claude Code and other AI IDEs so agents retain and retrieve knowledge across sessions. Founded by Da Nang native Duy Anh Nguyen, the team is based in Vietnam (US-incorporated), and its architecture is published in a technical paper ("Agent-Native Memory Through LLM-Curated Hierarchical Context").

**Today's interesting [article](https://technode.global/2026/07/30/discover-vietnam-tech-vietnam-tech-ecosystem-enters-quality-first-era-as-investors-bet-on-global-ambition/):** Backed by Ascend Vietnam Ventures (AVV), ByteRover is the poster child for AVV's thesis that Vietnam's early-stage flow is now clustering in agentic AI, enterprise SaaS and gaming. For NVIDIA, "memory" is the missing layer of the agentic stack — and a Da Nang pre-seed shipping a live Product Hunt launch plus an arXiv paper is precisely the research-grade origination signal Inception exists to catch early.

**Investors:** Ascend Vietnam Ventures (AVV)
