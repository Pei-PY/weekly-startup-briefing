### 1. ODC (ORAN Development Company)

**Vertical:** Telecom | **Total Raised to Date:** ~$47M+ | **Stage:** Series A | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Active user

**What we do:** ODC's Odyssey platform converts standard 5G base stations into distributed AI compute hubs — what the company calls "Token Factories." Rather than routing AI workloads to centralized data centers, ODC embeds inference capacity directly inside telecom infrastructure, enabling real-time AI at the radio access layer for autonomous systems, industrial robotics, and critical infrastructure applications.

**Today's interesting [article](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/odc-completes-a-45m-series-a-funding-architecting-the-global-distributed-compute-grid-to-power-the-ai-native-era-302725604.html):** ODC closed a $45M Series A with NVIDIA, Cisco Investments, Nokia, AT&T, MTN, Telecom Italia, and Booz Allen Hamilton all as named investors — a rare single-round coalition of the three dominant pillars of AI (compute, networking, telecom) plus defense. That investor syndicate is effectively a customer commitment list.

**Investors:** Booz Allen Hamilton, Cisco Investments, Nokia, NVIDIA, AT&T, MTN, Telecom Italia, Phoenix Venture Partners (lead), Cerberus Capital Management (seed)

**NVIDIA Relevancy:** ODC's Odyssey platform explicitly integrates the NVIDIA AI Aerial software stack for 5G AI-RAN capabilities — confirmed in the PR Newswire release. NVIDIA is also a named Series A investor, indicating strategic alignment at the infrastructure level. Spectrum-X and H100/B200 are both relevant as AI-RAN workloads scale.

### 7. Halter

### 10. CavilinQ

**Vertical:** Supercomputing / Quantum | **Total Raised to Date:** $8.8M | **Stage:** Seed | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟠 Potential

**What we do:** CavilinQ is building the interconnect layer for modular quantum computing — a scalable light-matter interface architecture that links separate quantum processors together into a unified, high-fidelity quantum machine. Rather than trying to build a single massive quantum chip, CavilinQ's approach allows utility-scale quantum systems to be assembled from smaller, higher-quality modules. The company is a spinout from the University of Chicago's Pritzker School of Molecular Engineering, co-founded by quantum faculty from both UChicago and Harvard.

**Today's interesting [article](https://polsky.uchicago.edu/2026/04/02/cavilinq-secures-8-8m-seed-round-to-unlock-utility-scale-quantum-computing/):** CavilinQ's scientific co-founders — Hannes Bernien (UChicago) and Mikhail Lukin (Harvard) — are two of the most-cited researchers in quantum networking globally, and the CEO Shankar Menon completed his PhD at UChicago in 2025. The Harper Court Ventures participation signals this is one of UChicago's highest-priority deep tech commercializations.

**Investors:** QVT / Quantonation (lead), Safar Partners, MFV Partners, Serendipity Capital, Harper Court Ventures (UChicago deep tech fund)

**NVIDIA Relevancy:** NVIDIA's cuQuantum library is designed precisely for GPU-accelerated quantum circuit simulation — the computational work needed to design, test, and validate quantum interconnects before physical implementation. As CavilinQ advances toward utility-scale quantum systems, classical GPU simulation of quantum network behavior (noise modeling, error correction, circuit optimization) may become a meaningful part of their R&D infrastructure.

## Interesting Trends

*Three macro signals emerging from today's news:*

1. **NVIDIA is moving from platform sponsor to direct co-investor at the infrastructure layer** — NVIDIA appeared as a named equity investor in two of today's top stories (ODC and Sarvam AI), in the same week NVentures confirmed 50+ deals in fiscal 2025. The pattern is consistent: NVIDIA invests in companies that are explicitly building on NVIDIA's tech stack (ODC on AI Aerial, Sarvam on H100-class infrastructure for sovereign AI). Inception teams should treat a new NVentures investment as a standing flag to check whether the company is already enrolled — and if not, to accelerate outreach before the relationship becomes a High Touch account.

2. **Agentic identity is the next Wiz-scale cybersecurity opportunity** — Linx Security's "Wiz for identity" positioning, combined with its rapid Series B at 18 months post-founding, reflects that the explosion of non-human and agentic identities (AI agents, API keys, service accounts) has created a new attack surface that legacy IAM tools cannot cover. NVIDIA's Morpheus platform is purpose-built for GPU-accelerated cybersecurity on high-volume event streams — this vertical is underrepresented in the current Inception pipeline and warrants a proactive sourcing push.

3. **Physical AI data infrastructure is emerging as a distinct fundable category** — Nomadic (AV data structuring), Sift (hardware telemetry — disqualified as High Touch), and ODC (distributed AI at the radio layer) all represent the same underlying thesis: the physical world is generating unstructured data faster than software can process it, and a new layer of GPU-accelerated data infrastructure is required to make physical AI tractable. Each of these companies is a natural fit for NVIDIA's edge-to-data-center stack — Jetson at the source, H100/B200 for centralized processing — and the category is early enough that Inception origination can still establish relationships pre-Series A.
