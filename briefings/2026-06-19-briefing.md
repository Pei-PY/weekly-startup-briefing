## Interesting Trends

1. **Week 25 wrap: $1.07B deployed into SEA AI ecosystem in 7 days** — GreatAsic ($6.9M), Lhoopa ($6M), YouLabs ($2.6M), Respond.io ($62.5M), Pints AI ($5.6M), Racks Central ($1B) — combined, this is the highest single-week capital deployment into Southeast Asia's technology ecosystem since Q3 2022's peak global VC market. The composition is different though: this week's deals are concentrated in AI infrastructure and AI-native applications rather than consumer internet.

2. **Government AI policy tailwinds are accelerating ASEAN startup commercialization timelines** — Five separate government policy actions this week (MAS AI governance framework, Singapore IMDA Digital Access Plan, Malaysia National Semiconductor Strategy Year 2, Singapore Food Security Masterplan, Indonesia JETP AI component) each created direct demand catalysts for specific companies in this briefing. The density of policy AI action is creating a government-driven enterprise sales motion for ASEAN AI startups that is unusual compared to the US market where regulatory action is primarily restrictive rather than enabling.

3. **NVIDIA's positioning in SEA is strongest in physical AI and data center infrastructure; weakest in fintech AI** — This week's deal flow highlights where NVIDIA is well-positioned (Racks Central infrastructure, Polybee drones, Muun AI factory edge, Video Rebirth video generation) and where the competitive AI ecosystem is advancing without NVIDIA (Pints AI on standard cloud, Respond.io on AWS/GCP, Level3AI undisclosed). The Fintech/FSI vertical in SEA is the largest revenue-potential market without a clear NVIDIA story — RAPIDS and Morpheus are the products, but the GTM is underdeveloped for Singapore's MAS-regulated financial sector.
