### 1. Featherless.ai
**Vertical:** AI Developer Tools / CRISP | **Total Raised to Date:** ~$20M+ | **Stage:** Series A | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Serverless inference for 30,000+ open models

**What we do:** Featherless.ai runs a serverless AI inference platform that lets developers call any of 30,000+ open-source models — language, vision, and audio — on demand, without ever provisioning a GPU. It's a production-ready alternative to closed API providers: point it at a Hugging Face model and Featherless turns it into a hosted, auto-scaled endpoint. Co-founded in Singapore by CEO Eugene Cheah, whose team authored the open-source RWKV architecture, the company abstracts away GPU orchestration and multi-model scheduling so that "run any open model in production" becomes a single API call.

**Today's interesting [article](https://technode.global/2026/05/01/singapore-co-founded-ai-firm-featherless-ai-raises-20m-series-a-funding/):** Featherless.ai raised a $20M Series A (May 2026) co-led by **AMD Ventures** and Airbus Ventures, with BMW i Ventures, Kickstart, Panache and Wavemaker joining. The strategic read for NVIDIA Inception: Featherless is one of the purest "GPU-cloud-as-a-business" plays in Southeast Asia — its unit economics *are* inference cost-per-token across a 30,000-model catalog. The AMD Ventures lead is a competitive flag worth surfacing: AMD is trying to anchor a high-volume inference platform on its MI-series accelerators before it standardizes on CUDA. Winning — or keeping — Featherless on the NVIDIA inference stack is exactly the kind of early, high-leverage account the Inception program exists to capture.

**Investors:** AMD Ventures (co-lead), Airbus Ventures (co-lead), BMW i Ventures, Kickstart Ventures, Panache Ventures, Wavemaker Ventures

**NVIDIA Relevancy:** Featherless's entire business is high-throughput, multi-tenant inference across tens of thousands of models — the canonical workload for **TensorRT-LLM**, **Triton**, and **Dynamo** (multi-model serving with dynamic batching, model hot-swapping, and KV-cache-aware routing), and **NIM** microservices. Serving 30,000 models cost-effectively is fundamentally a memory-and-scheduling problem that **Dynamo**'s disaggregated serving and **NIM**'s optimized runtimes could potentially target, with **TensorRT Model Optimizer** relevant to per-model quantization. The AMD Ventures lead makes this a competitive-retention account: NVIDIA's inference software stack (TensorRT-LLM + Dynamo) remains the price/performance reference for exactly this serverless pattern, though there is no public proof of the silicon Featherless runs today.

**NVIDIA Case Study:** **Together AI / DeepInfra** — leading serverless inference providers serving open-source models cut cost-per-token sharply on NVIDIA's inference software stack (TensorRT-LLM, Dynamo, NVFP4): DeepInfra dropped from 20¢ to 5¢ per million tokens (~4×) and Together AI cut cost-per-query ~6× — the exact multi-model, open-weights serving economics Featherless is built on ([case study](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/inference-open-source-models-blackwell-reduce-cost-per-token/) · via site search — AI Developer Tools maps by inference-provider use case)
