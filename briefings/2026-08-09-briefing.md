### 1. Float16

**Vertical:** AI Developer Tools | **Total Raised to Date:** Undisclosed (seed) | **Stage:** Seed | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**What we do:** Float16 is a Bangkok-based full-stack GPU management platform offering the "cheapest serverless GPU" in spot mode and "fastest GPU endpoint" in deploy mode, purpose-built to make Thai startups GPU-rich. Its serverless GPU scales to zero with sub-10-second cold starts (~5s average), and it has published advanced optimization techniques for non-English LLMs that reach pricing parity with Together.ai. It also ships Thai OCR and Thai LLM tooling and co-built the first 70B Thai LLM.

**Today's interesting [article](https://float16.cloud/):** Sunday infra-AI focus: Float16 is one of the very few SEA startups whose entire product *is* GPU inference economics — and its platform site states it is "supported by NVIDIA and Typhoon (SCB10X)." For NVIDIA, a serverless-GPU layer optimized for Thai/Southeast-Asian languages sitting on top of NVIDIA silicon is exactly the ecosystem partner that pulls the region's long tail of developers onto CUDA-native inference rather than vendor-hosted APIs.

**Investors:** SCB 10X / Typhoon (strategic ecosystem), seed investors undisclosed

**NVIDIA Relevancy:** Verified: Float16's platform site states it is supported by NVIDIA and SCB10X's Typhoon, and its serverless GPU product self-hosts LLMs on NVIDIA GPUs (per its "which GPU to self-host LLM" guide). Its inference stack maps to TensorRT-LLM (in-flight/dynamic batching), Triton and Dynamo (serverless multi-model serving, KV-cache-aware routing), NeMo (Thai LLM fine-tuning), and NeMo Retriever plus an OCR NIM for its Thai OCR path.

**NVIDIA Case Study:** **Amdocs** — deployed domain-tuned LLMs on NIM and measured 4–6× faster inference, ~80% lower latency, and up to 60% fewer tokens consumed — the exact serving-economics profile Float16 sells to its serverless-GPU customers ([case study](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/amdocs-accelerates-generative-ai-performance-and-lowers-costs-with-nvidia-nim/) · via [NVIDIA telecommunications page](https://www.nvidia.com/en-sg/industries/telecommunications/), AI Developer Tools maps by customer served)

### 2. TeamSolve

**Vertical:** Manufacturing & Industrial | **Total Raised to Date:** ~$2.5M | **Stage:** Seed | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**What we do:** TeamSolve builds a generative-AI "Knowledge Twin" Co-Pilot for industrial and utilities operators — an accurate, real-time view of asset health that makes troubleshooting knowledge accessible to frontline workers via familiar messaging apps like WhatsApp. The Co-Pilot learns on the job from field staff through text, image, and audio input, and applies across commercial buildings, industrial facilities, and utilities. Founded 2022 in Singapore; completed a pilot with Singapore's Public Utilities Board (PUB) Water Supply Network Department.

**Today's interesting [article](https://technode.global/2024/10/01/generative-ai-startup-teamsolve-raises-seed-round-backed-by-sginnovate-burnt-island-ventures/):** Sunday industrial-AI read: TeamSolve's $2.5M seed (SGInnovate, Burnt Island Ventures) funds one of SEA's clearest "GenAI for the physical world" plays — a multimodal RAG copilot deployed against real water-utility assets, not a chatbot demo. The PUB pilot matters because water and power utilities are exactly the regulated, on-prem-first buyers where NVIDIA's sovereign/enterprise inference stack has a durable advantage over cloud APIs.

**Investors:** SGInnovate, Burnt Island Ventures

**NVIDIA Relevancy:** TeamSolve's multimodal Knowledge Twin maps to NeMo Retriever (embedding/reranking NIMs for asset documentation), cuVS (vector search over maintenance corpora), NIM LLM microservices and TensorRT-LLM for on-prem utility deployments, Riva (audio field-note transcription), and NeMo Guardrails for safe operator guidance; Metropolis/DeepStream could align with image-based asset inspection.

## Interesting Trends

1. **Thailand's SCB10X/Typhoon ecosystem is quietly building a full sovereign-AI stack — infra to apps.** Float16 (serverless GPU + 70B Thai LLM, "supported by NVIDIA and Typhoon") and Zwiz.AI (Thai chatbot + slip OCR) show two layers of the same thesis: Thailand is assembling both the GPU-serving infrastructure and the Thai-language application layer that sits on it. For NVIDIA, Float16 is the highest-leverage relationship in today's ten — an ecosystem partner that funnels the region's developer long tail onto CUDA-native inference rather than foreign APIs.

2. **The Philippines' DOST-TAPI GITEX cohort is a government-curated physical-AI pipeline.** Three of today's companies (GreenVisions, GradeChum, Cawil.AI) trace to Philippine state innovation programs, and each attacks a hard *local* problem with edge inference — offline soil/disease diagnostics in Mindanao, tri-lingual handwriting OCR, and bridge/flood-control CV. Grant-stage and undisclosed-funding as expected, but the use cases (edge vision, local-language OCR) are squarely NVIDIA edge-AI territory and worth watching as they commercialize post-GITEX.

3. **"AI-enabled vertical SaaS" and deep-tech materials bracket the maturity spectrum this week.** At one end, mature revenue-generating SaaS (FeedMe's 11,000 merchants, Zwiz's 2017 base, E3A's 500K babies) is layering AI onto proprietary transaction/sensor data — the data is the moat, GPU-accelerated ML the upgrade path. At the other, Qarbotech is a nanomaterials play whose only NVIDIA angle is prospective simulation. The takeaway for Inception: the strongest near-term fits are the CV/edge and inference-infra companies (Float16, Cawil.AI, TeamSolve, E3A), while grant-stage and materials plays warrant a watch-and-nurture posture.
