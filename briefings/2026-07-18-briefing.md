## Interesting Trends

1. **SEA's frontier deep-tech is simulation- and EDA-bound, not just LLM-bound** — Zero-Error Systems (rad-hard IC verification), Aliena (Hall-effect plasma/CFD), and Nuevocor (mechanobiology target discovery) all bottleneck on physics simulation, EDA, and genomics — the CUDA-X HPC / CUDA-X EDA / BioNeMo / Parabricks side of NVIDIA's platform, not the generative-LLM side. These are the highest-leverage Inception conversations because the acceleration gap is quantifiable (30–110× documented) and the teams are small enough that a single GPU-flow adoption reshapes their timeline.

2. **The deepfake-KYC arms race is a GPU-native inference market forming in real time** — Verihubs (pixel/frame deepfake detection) and Nodeflux (face recognition, national-ID matching) are both scaling always-on computer-vision inference against Indonesia's new biometric-liveness mandates and generative-fraud surge. This is Metropolis/DeepStream/TensorRT territory, and the regulatory tailwind converts a "nice to have" into required, recurring inference spend across SEA banks and governments.

3. **Climate risk is pulling insurtech into weather-simulation compute** — RuralNet's Q3 2026 parametric calamity cover and Verta Bioenergy's decarbonization offtake both hinge on physical-world modeling: parametric flood/typhoon triggers map directly to NVIDIA Earth-2/FourCastNet, while industrial decarbonization maps to cuOpt logistics and Omniverse plant twins. As SEA's most climate-exposed populations get insured products, the actuarial layer moves from spreadsheets toward AI weather ensembles — a new NVIDIA-adjacent workload in the region's fintech stack.
