### 1. Augmentus

**Vertical:** Robotics | **Total Raised to Date:** ~$16M (incl. $11M Series A+) | **Stage:** Series A+ | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**What we do:** Augmentus builds a no-code robotics platform that combines industrial 3D scanning, AI-driven path planning, and adaptive motion control to generate robot programs in minutes rather than weeks — targeting high-mix, low-volume manufacturing (welding, sanding, spraying, coating). Singapore HQ, founded 2019. 50+ systems deployed across six countries, with customers reporting up to 90% reduction in programming time and robot downtime cut from hours to minutes.

**Today's interesting [article](https://www.therobotreport.com/augmentus-raises-series-a-funding-to-reduce-robot-programming-complexity/):** Augmentus's $11M Series A+ (led by Woori Ventures, with Singapore's EDBI joining alongside Sierra Ventures and Cocoon Capital) is one of the largest SEA robotics-software rounds of 2025 and a sovereign-state signal — EDBI's participation ties the company to Singapore's advanced-manufacturing agenda. For NVIDIA, a no-code robot-programming layer that abstracts away toolpath generation is exactly the application tier that pulls Isaac Sim and cuMotion into every deployment; this is a high-leverage, GPU-native Inception account with no competing accelerator ecosystem on the cap table.

**Investors:** Woori Ventures (lead), EDBI, Sierra Ventures, Cocoon Capital

**NVIDIA Relevancy:** Isaac Sim and Isaac Lab could align with simulating and validating auto-generated toolpaths before deployment; cuMotion (Isaac Manipulator) is relevant to GPU-accelerated collision-free motion planning; Isaac ROS (cuVSLAM, nvblox) and JetPack map to the on-robot perception and 3D-scan pipeline; Omniverse Replicator could generate synthetic training data for new part geometries; TensorRT would optimize the vision-inference stage of 3D scanning.

**NVIDIA Case Study:** **Volkswagen Sachsen (via Wandelbots)** — used the hardware-agnostic Wandelbots NOVA platform on NVIDIA Isaac Sim to virtually program, simulate, and deploy robots in physically accurate environments, cutting robot programming from weeks to minutes for complex automotive tasks — the same no-code, scan-to-program value proposition Augmentus sells to high-mix manufacturers ([case study](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/case-studies/volkswagen-with-wandelbots-nova-and-isaac-sim/) · via [NVIDIA robotics page](https://www.nvidia.com/en-sg/industries/robotics/))

### 3. CiRA CORE

**Vertical:** AI Developer Tools | **Total Raised to Date:** Undisclosed | **Stage:** Early-stage / spinout | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**What we do:** CiRA CORE is a low-code/no-code AI integrated development environment for machine vision, model training, and robot/automation control — built by the Center of Industrial Robot and Automatic (CiRA) at King Mongkut's Institute of Technology Ladkrabang (KMITL), Thailand. Its drag-and-drop block interface integrates YOLO and ROS, letting users build image-recognition and Industry 4.0 automation pipelines without deep coding. Deployed in Thai manufacturing, smart-city, and educational settings (free for education).

**Today's interesting [article](https://www.chiangraitimes.com/ai/thailands-most-promising-ai-startups/):** CiRA CORE is a rare university-born, sovereign-Thai machine-vision platform gaining traction through a commercial arm ("CiRA Automation and Technology" via TKK Corporation) — a spinout-to-commercialization path NVIDIA Inception is designed to catch early. Because the platform bundles YOLO training and vision inference into its blocks, every industrial deployment is a latent TAO Toolkit / TensorRT / DeepStream workload; the question is whether the CiRA ecosystem standardizes on CUDA-accelerated training as it scales beyond KMITL's labs.

**Investors:** Not disclosed (KMITL / CiRA spinout; commercialized via TKK Corporation)

## Interesting Trends

1. **Sovereign, own-silicon AI is a real SEA category — and Thailand is leading it.** iApp Technology runs its **own GPU datacenter in Thailand** (via True IDC colocation) to train and self-host the 72B OpenThaiGPT, while Vulcan Coalition and CiRA CORE build Thai-language and Thai machine-vision AI from local data supply chains. Three of today's ten are Thai AI-infrastructure plays betting that data residency and local-language quality are defensible — exactly the on-shore NeMo/TensorRT-LLM/Riva footprint NVIDIA's sovereign-AI motion targets.

2. **No-code/low-code is where SEA's GPU-native applications actually live.** Augmentus (scan-to-toolpath robotics), CiRA CORE (block-based vision), and Elgo AI (drag-and-drop private GenAI apps) all abstract away programming — and in doing so become the application tier that silently pulls Isaac Sim, cuMotion, TAO Toolkit, and NIM into every deployment. The highest-leverage Inception engagement in this cohort is Augmentus: Series A+, GPU-native, EDBI-backed, and no competing accelerator ecosystem on the cap table.

3. **The "weak-AI / undisclosed-funding" tail is the honest majority of early SEA.** Six of today's ten (Elgo, CiRA CORE, Vulcan, Paypers, Innov Smarter, plus founder-funded iApp) carry undisclosed capital, and several — Okapi (solar financing), Innov Smarter (pilot-stage precision ag), HealthNow (consumer telehealth) — are financing- or app-first with limited current compute. This is the expected shape of a pre-seed-heavy accelerator cohort: rate the compute reality honestly (🟠), but keep a watch-list on the ones whose roadmaps (clinical patient agents, solar forecasting, satellite crop analytics) map cleanly onto known NVIDIA acceleration patterns once they scale.
