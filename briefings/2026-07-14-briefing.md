### 1. SynaXG

**Vertical:** Telecom | **Total Raised to Date:** ~$20M | **Stage:** Seed | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**What we do:** SynaXG builds AI-native radio access network (AI-RAN) infrastructure — a software-defined RAN that runs 5G/6G baseband and AI workloads on the same accelerated compute, targeting telcos and enterprise private networks. Singapore HQ, founded by wireless-industry veteran Xin Huang. The company has already run 24+ hour carrier-grade energy-efficiency tests with Keysight and Arm, and demonstrated the world's first mmWave 5G virtualized RAN (vRAN) on the NVIDIA AI Aerial platform.

**Today's interesting [article](https://technode.global/2025/12/03/singapores-synaxg-raises-over-20m-funding-to-accelerate-global-ai-ran/):** SynaXG's ~US$20M first round — led by January Capital, Vertex Ventures and Qualgro — is one of Asia's largest early-stage AI-RAN bets, landing exactly as the AI-RAN category goes commercial (Nokia + NVIDIA announced the first GPU-based AI-RAN targeting double spectrum capacity on July 16, 2026). For NVIDIA this is the highest-fit SEA deal in this briefing: a startup that is already building directly on NVIDIA AI Aerial, converging RAN and AI on one GPU fabric — the exact thesis SoftBank has been proving with NVIDIA.

**Investors:** January Capital (lead), Vertex Ventures Southeast Asia & India, Qualgro

**NVIDIA Relevancy:** Verified NVIDIA usage: SynaXG demonstrated the world's first mmWave 5G vRAN on the **NVIDIA AI Aerial** platform per its funding announcement. Its L1/PHY stack maps to Aerial CUDA-Accelerated RAN (cuBB/cuPHY, cuMAC); AI-for-RAN research and channel modeling to Sionna and pyAerial; network-model training to NeMo, Megatron-Core and TensorRT; digital-twin RAN validation to the Aerial Omniverse Digital Twin; and converged "AI-and-RAN" multi-tenant serving to NIM, Triton and TensorRT-LLM.

**NVIDIA Case Study:** **SoftBank** — built AITRAS, an AI-native RAN on NVIDIA AI Aerial, delivering up to ~2× throughput and higher spectral and energy efficiency from the same spectrum, with a 16-layer outdoor Massive MU-MIMO trial run fully in software on GPU — the direct large-operator analog to SynaXG's AI-RAN thesis ([case study](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/maximize-spectral-efficiency-with-ai-native-ran-and-nvidia-ai-aerial) · via [NVIDIA telecommunications page](https://www.nvidia.com/en-sg/industries/telecommunications/))

**Vertical:** Healthcare & Life Sciences | **Total Raised to Date:** ~$19M | **Stage:** Series A | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

Singapore HQ, with regulatory approvals across 28+ markets.
