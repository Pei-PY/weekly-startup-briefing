### 1. Kumi Analytics

**Vertical:** Space | **Total Raised to Date:** Undisclosed (accelerator-backed) | **Stage:** Seed | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**What we do:** Kumi Analytics is a Singapore geospatial-intelligence company providing audit-grade digital monitoring, reporting and verification (dMRV) for nature-based carbon projects, combining satellite imagery with deep learning to set conservation baselines and track blue-carbon and forest projects. Founded in 2018; it partnered with NUS's Centre for Remote Imaging, Sensing and Processing in 2025 on blue-carbon methodologies.

**Today's interesting [article](https://technode.global/2026/09/07/google-deepmind-picks-16-environmental-ai-projects-across-asia-pacific-for-inaugural-accelerator/):** On September 7, 2026 Kumi Analytics was named one of 16 projects — and one of three from Singapore — in Google DeepMind's inaugural AI for the Planet accelerator for Asia-Pacific, a three-month program with a Singapore bootcamp, model access and cloud credits, no equity taken.

**Investors:** Seraphim Space (accelerator), Hatch Blue, Singapore Economic Development Board programmes

**NVIDIA Relevancy:** Satellite-imagery segmentation and change detection could align with TAO Toolkit and TensorRT for inference over large scenes; RAPIDS cuSpatial may be relevant to geospatial joins across project boundaries; Omniverse could apply to visualising carbon-project digital twins for auditors.

**NVIDIA Case Study:** **Planet Labs** — processes satellite imagery with GPU-accelerated deep learning for land-cover analytics, an analog for Kumi's conservation baselining ([case study](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/industries/) · via [NVIDIA industries page](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/industries/))

### 2. dtcpay

**Vertical:** FinTech / FSI | **Total Raised to Date:** ~$41.5M | **Stage:** Series A | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟠 Maybe

**What we do:** dtcpay (Digital Treasures Center) is a Singapore payments company that lets merchants accept stablecoins and digital assets and settle in fiat, with a business portal for enterprise clients and a consumer app. Founded in 2019 by NUS classmates Alice Liu (CEO) and Band Zhao (Chairman); MAS-licensed.

**Today's interesting [article](https://technode.global/2026/09/18/singapores-dtcpay-extends-25m-series-a-funding-round-with-sbi-investment/):** On September 18, 2026 dtcpay extended its Series A to $25M with SBI Group as strategic investor (via SBI Ventures Asset and the SBI-NTU-Kyobo Digital Innovation Fund), following the Vertex Ventures-led initial tranche in March. Genedant Capital and existing backer Kwee Liong Tek also participated; funds go to the 2026 product roadmap.

**Investors:** Vertex Ventures Southeast Asia & India (initial lead), SBI Group, Genedant Capital, Kwee Liong Tek

**NVIDIA Relevancy:** Payment routing and settlement are transaction workloads; Morpheus could potentially align with real-time fraud and AML screening across stablecoin flows, and RAPIDS may be relevant to treasury and FX analytics as volumes scale. No GPU-native workload is evident today.

## Interesting Trends

1. **Exits, not rounds, defined mid-September** — Circle's $400M all-stock purchase of Tazapay and Grab's $1.49B move on Atome show US and regional acquirers paying up for Singapore payments infrastructure, which will recycle founder and employee capital into the next seed cohort.
2. **Japanese strategics keep arriving** — Ricoh's first venture-fund deal (Intellect) and SBI's dtcpay extension continue August's pattern; the common thread is distribution into Japan in exchange for data partnerships.
3. **Climate and nature AI is being incubated by Big Tech programmes rather than VC** — Google DeepMind's accelerator picked six SEA projects (Kumi, SIGMA, City Syntax Lab, Living Roots, Edufarmers, Yayasan Ekosistem Lestari); compute for these companies arrives as cloud credits, so GPU access is tied to hyperscaler programmes.
