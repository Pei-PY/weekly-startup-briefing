### 1. Hertzwell

**Vertical:** Auto / AV Infra | **Total Raised to Date:** ~$3.4M | **Stage:** Series A | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**What we do:** Hertzwell builds a hyper-resolution 4D imaging-radar perception stack — computational imaging plus deep learning that transforms low-resolution radar returns into dense, high-resolution point clouds, letting autonomous vehicles and robots "see" through rain, fog, dust, and glare where cameras and LiDAR degrade. The software is chip-agnostic and pairs with camera-radar fusion. Singapore HQ (Entrepreneurs First / SGInnovate deep-tech origin), founded 2017. Holds a top position on the nuScenes 3D object-detection leaderboard.

**Today's interesting [article](https://app.dealroom.co/news/feed/hertzwell-raises-2-4m-for-radar-tech):** Monday AV-infra spotlight: Hertzwell's $2.4M Series A (Vynn Capital, SGInnovate, Atlantic Labs) funds a software-defined radar approach that undercuts LiDAR cost while topping a public autonomy benchmark — the rare SEA deep-tech company competing directly on the nuScenes leaderboard. For NVIDIA, radar-to-point-cloud perception is squarely a DRIVE/DriveWorks and TensorRT in-vehicle-inference workload, making this a greenfield AV-perception account in a region where DRIVE ecosystem seeding is still early.

**Investors:** Vynn Capital, SGInnovate, Atlantic Labs, Entrepreneurs First (prior), angel investors

**NVIDIA Relevancy:** Radar signal-to-point-cloud deep learning maps to DriveWorks (CUDA point-cloud accumulation/registration) and TensorRT for in-vehicle inference; DRIVE Sim / Omniverse could align with synthetic radar-scene generation and radar-model validation; camera-radar fusion training on GPU clusters maps to CUDA, cuDNN, NCCL and TAO Toolkit for detection-model transfer learning. Chip-agnostic deployment keeps prospective tooling software-only pending disclosure of the target compute platform.

## Interesting Trends

1. **Benchmark-topping deep tech is emerging from SEA at seed/Series-A — and it is GPU-native.** Hertzwell holds a top nuScenes 3D object-detection position on a ~$3.4M Series A, and Datature's no-code CV platform (medical/manufacturing/agriculture) is entirely an annotate-train-deploy GPU loop. Both are the kind of DRIVE/DriveWorks and TAO/TensorRT accounts NVIDIA would rather seed at Series A than meet at scale — the highest-leverage Inception targets in this issue.

2. **Sovereign and vernacular AI is a distinct SEA model-builder lane.** Arcanic AI (Vietnamese Cono LLMs), Mandala AI (Thai social listening), and Ecomobi (multilingual creator/content AI) all build language-specific stacks for markets frontier models underserve. That maps directly to NeMo (fine-tuning/curation), Riva (local-language speech), and TensorRT-LLM/NIM serving — a repeatable "vernacular-AI on NVIDIA software" thesis distinct from the English-first frontier race.

3. **CleanTech and e-mobility raise the most capital but score lowest on NVIDIA fit — the AI is in the data layer, not the core.** Xurya (~$90M total) and Swap Energy (~$22M Series A) are the best-funded companies here, yet both are asset/network businesses where NVIDIA relevance is confined to RAPIDS forecasting, cuOpt optimization, and cuML battery/plant health prediction. The pattern: in SEA climate tech, Inception's opening is the analytics/optimization layer bolted onto physical infrastructure, not the infrastructure itself.
