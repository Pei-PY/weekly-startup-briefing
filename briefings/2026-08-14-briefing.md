### 1. SixSense

**Vertical:** Supercomputing / Quantum (Semiconductors) | **Total Raised to Date:** ~$12M | **Stage:** Series A | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**What we do:** SixSense builds an AI computer-vision platform that predicts and detects defects on semiconductor production lines in real time — automating visual inspection, lot disposition, and root-cause analysis for fabs, OSATs, and IDMs. Singapore HQ, founded 2018 by Akanksha Jagwani (CEO) and Avni Agrawal (CTO). Deployed at GlobalFoundries and JCET; has analysed 100M+ chips, lifting classification accuracy to ~98%, cutting manual inspection ~90%, and raising yield 1–2%.

**Today's interesting [article](https://techcrunch.com/2025/07/31/female-founded-semiconductor-ai-startup-sixsense-raises-funding/):** Heading into H2 2026, SixSense is the clearest NVIDIA-adjacent semiconductor AI story in SEA: its $8.5M Series A (led by Peak XV, with Alpha Intelligence Capital and FEBE) funds expansion across Singapore, Malaysia, Taiwan, and Israel — precisely the fabrication corridor NVIDIA's supply chain runs through. A female-founded, deep-learning vision company embedded inside GlobalFoundries and JCET's inspection flow is exactly the kind of edge-to-datacenter CV workload NVIDIA's manufacturing stack was built to accelerate.

**Investors:** Peak XV Partners (lead), Alpha Intelligence Capital, FEBE Ventures

**NVIDIA Relevancy:** SixSense's defect-detection and classification models map to TensorRT / TensorRT-LLM for optimized inference on the line, DeepStream and Metropolis for streaming visual-inspection analytics, TAO Toolkit for transfer-learning on new wafer/package classes, DALI + CV-CUDA for high-throughput image preprocessing, and Triton for serving no-code CV models across fabs. RAPIDS cuML could accelerate the root-cause and yield-regression analytics behind lot disposition.

**NVIDIA Case Study:** **Pegatron** — the electronics manufacturer built visual-AI inspection agents and digital twins on Metropolis, TAO Toolkit, and Isaac Sim, accelerating AI-agent development ~400% over four years and automating defect detection on the line — the reference architecture for an automated wafer/package inspection platform like SixSense ([case study](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/case-studies/pegatron-scales-factory-operations-with-visual-ai-digital-twins/) · via [NVIDIA manufacturing page](https://www.nvidia.com/en-sg/industries/manufacturing/))

### 2. MaiStorage Technology

**Vertical:** Supercomputing / Quantum (Semiconductors) | **Total Raised to Date:** ~$22M (RM100M corporate seed) | **Stage:** Seed (corporate, Phison-backed) | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**What we do:** MaiStorage is a Phison Group-backed IC-design and storage company building NAND SSD solutions and the aiDAPTIV+ on-premises LLM training/fine-tuning platform in Malaysia. Its aiDAPTIV+ stack pairs SSD-based memory extension with GPUs so organisations can fine-tune very large models on far less VRAM — Phison has demonstrated fine-tuning Llama 3.1 405B on a single server with two GPUs and 192GB VRAM (a task that normally needs 7TB+ of pooled VRAM across multiple NVIDIA servers). Kuala Lumpur / Cyberjaya HQ; trained 60 IC-design engineers toward a 175-engineer 2026 target.

**Today's interesting [article](https://www.crnasia.com/news/2025/components-and-peripherals/maistorage-technology-launches-in-malaysia-to-drive-innovati):** MaiStorage sits at the centre of Malaysia's sovereign-AI push heading into H2 2026: its aiDAPTIV+ Partner Program is explicitly positioned to reduce reliance on foreign cloud and enable on-prem, in-country LLM fine-tuning across ASEAN. Because aiDAPTIV+ is a memory-extension layer that runs *on top of* GPUs, MaiStorage is one of the few SEA companies whose product directly amplifies the economics of NVIDIA training silicon rather than competing with it.

**Investors:** Phison Group (corporate parent / strategic), MIMOS (national R&D)

**NVIDIA Relevancy:** Verified NVIDIA usage: Phison's aiDAPTIV+ reference demonstrations fine-tune large models on NVIDIA GPUs (its 405B demo is benchmarked against pooled NVIDIA-server VRAM), and aiDAPTIV+ appliances commonly ship with NVIDIA professional GPUs. On the software side, the fine-tuning pipeline maps to NeMo Framework (Megatron-Core, LoRA/PEFT recipes), Transformer Engine (FP8), and NCCL for multi-GPU scaling; served on-prem models map to TensorRT-LLM and NIM microservices; NVAIE provides the certified enterprise stack for sovereign deployments.

**NVIDIA Case Study:** **ServiceNow** — co-developed the Apriel Nemotron domain models on NeMo and NIM, delivering purpose-built enterprise LLMs at a fraction of frontier-model training spend — the same on-prem, cost-controlled fine-tuning outcome aiDAPTIV+ sells to Malaysian and ASEAN enterprises ([case study](https://www.servicenow.com/company/media/press-room/nvidia-agentic-ai-partnership.html) · via [NVIDIA supercomputing page](https://www.nvidia.com/en-sg/industries/supercomputing/))

## Interesting Trends

1. **SEA's semiconductor-AI layer is maturing on both sides of the GPU** — SixSense (inline wafer-defect CV, 100M+ chips analysed at GlobalFoundries/JCET) and MaiStorage (Phison-backed aiDAPTIV+ on-prem LLM fine-tuning on NVIDIA GPUs) show the region building AI *for* chips and AI-training infrastructure *from* chips simultaneously. Both are complementary to NVIDIA's stack — one accelerates fab inspection with TensorRT/Metropolis, the other extends the economics of NVIDIA training silicon — making the Singapore–Malaysia semiconductor corridor a priority Inception cluster for H2 2026.

2. **Sovereign genomics is SEA's most defensible life-sciences moat** — GeneStory (42M+ variants from Vingroup's VN1K, published in *Nature Communications*, running on Vietnam's NVIDIA DGX SuperPOD) and Singrow (reverse-genetics crop breeding) both convert population- or crop-specific genomic data into GPU-native pipelines that map cleanly to Parabricks and BioNeMo. Data sovereignty — human and agricultural — is emerging as the durable wedge that global players cannot easily replicate, and every step of it is accelerable.

3. **Embedded fintech keeps riding on top of another product** — LenderLink (real-time credit bureau, 36M+ borrower records), Pintarnya (jobs platform + asset-backed lending), and even AIYA (chat-commerce + CRM) share a pattern: the fintech/risk layer is bolted onto a data-rich primary product, and its scoring/fraud core is a RAPIDS/Morpheus-shaped workload. As record and transaction volumes compound in Indonesia and the Philippines, real-time credit-risk computation is the workload most likely to force these software-first companies onto accelerated data science.
