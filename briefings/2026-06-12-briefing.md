## Interesting Trends

1. **Post-SuperAI deal flow is lagging but accelerating** — SuperAI Singapore concluded June 11 with 10,000 attendees and 1,500 companies, but the pattern from previous Singapore AI events (Token2049, AI Expo Asia) is that deal announcements surface 2–4 weeks after the event as conversations at booth-side convert to signed term sheets. Watch June 23–July 5 as the primary announcement window for SuperAI-catalyzed deals.

2. **Longevity health AI is SEA's emerging healthcare wedge** — YouLabs' $2.6M seed targeting South Asian longevity data represents a category that is underserved by existing AI health platforms. The South Asian population's specific genetic risk profile (T2D, cardiovascular, metabolic syndrome) differs enough from Western longevity datasets to make a dedicated data company viable. If YouLabs can build the "South Asian longevity genome," it becomes a dataset moat rather than just a product company.

3. **Video AI is the most active GPU compute battlefield in SEA** — Video Rebirth (AMD Ventures-backed) vs. NVIDIA's broader video AI ecosystem is the clearest GPU competitor battleground in Southeast Asia right now. AMD investing in Video Rebirth is a direct signal that AMD is trying to capture the video AI inference stack in ASEAN before NVIDIA establishes NIM as the default inference serving layer. This is a conversation NVIDIA Inception should have with Video Rebirth urgently.
