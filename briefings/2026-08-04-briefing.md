### 1. Videotto

**Vertical:** Media & Entertainment | **Total Raised to Date:** Undisclosed (Seed) | **Stage:** Seed | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**What we do:** Videotto is an AI-native video-editing agent that turns long-form videos (podcasts, webinars, streams) into short, platform-optimized viral clips — identifying key moments, cutting, captioning and reformatting automatically, reportedly compressing what took ~20 hours of manual editing into minutes. Singapore HQ, founded 2025 by an 18-year-old duo (Tay Yao Ming, CEO; Ian Lee, CTO) running a four-person "strike team." Debuted at Singapore's SWITCH 2025.

**Today's interesting [article](https://technode.global/2025/10/29/east-ventures-invests-in-singapores-ai-native-video-editing-startup-videotto/):** East Ventures — one of SEA's most active seed investors — backing a teenage-founded, AI-native video company is a signal worth tracking: video generation and understanding is among the most GPU-hungry consumer-AI workloads, and Videotto's "~100× faster clipping" pitch is entirely inference-bound. For NVIDIA in SEA, this is exactly the greenfield, GPU-native seed profile where Inception engagement compounds — no incumbent GPU-ecosystem relationship, a subscription model that creates recurring inference demand, and a use case (temporal video segmentation + captioning) that maps cleanly onto the media-AI stack.

**Investors:** East Ventures

**NVIDIA Relevancy:** Software-only mapping: video-understanding and scene/highlight detection could align with DeepStream and the DALI + Video Codec SDK decode pipeline; TensorRT and Triton/Dynamo are relevant to serving diffusion/transcription models at low latency; Riva (ASR) and NeMo could power auto-captioning and multilingual transcription; Maxine may apply to speaker framing and enhancement. No public evidence of the current silicon vendor — likely cloud-hosted GPU inference (undisclosed).

**Vertical:** Adtech / Martech | **Total Raised to Date:** Undisclosed (Early-stage) | **Stage:** Early-stage / Seed | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**Today's interesting [article](https://botika.online/):** Botika's differentiation is language sovereignty: a natural, native Bahasa Indonesia voice and NLP layer for a 270M-person market that global conversational-AI vendors under-serve. For NVIDIA, Botika is a clean fit for the local-language speech + agentic-CX pattern that Riva and NeMo were built for.

### 3. WEHLO

**Vertical:** Energy / CleanTech | **Total Raised to Date:** ~PHP15M (~$260K) DOST-PCIEERD grant | **Stage:** Grant-funded / Pre-Seed | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**What we do:** WEHLO (Localized Weather, Environment, and Hydromet) Resilience Technology Corp. runs a hyperlocal environmental-monitoring platform — 24/7 real-time sensor data on temperature, humidity, rainfall, wind and air quality — layered with "Vertical AI" models that fuse ground-sensor data, topography and historical patterns to predict flood paths and landslide risk at the barangay (village) level. A Mapúa University spin-out (School of Civil, Environmental & Geological Engineering, led by Dr. Francis Aldrine Uy), Manila, Philippines. Selected for GITEX AI Asia 2026.

**Today's interesting [article](https://www.bworldonline.com/technology/2026/03/19/737161/7-filipino-ai-startups-head-to-gitex-ai-asia/):** WEHLO is the clearest physics-ML fit in this briefing: hyperlocal flood/landslide prediction from sensor + topography fusion is precisely the workload NVIDIA's Earth-2 and FourCastNet were built to accelerate — traditional numerical weather prediction takes hours, AI emulators take seconds. For a disaster-prone archipelago, WEHLO commercializing barangay-level forecasting via a DOST grant is a textbook sovereign-climate-AI use case, and one where NVIDIA's weather stack is the reference architecture rather than a stretch.

**Investors:** DOST-PCIEERD (grant); DOST-TAPI (commercialization support)

**NVIDIA Relevancy:** Software-only mapping: Earth-2 platform components (FourCastNet, CorrDiff downscaling) and Modulus (physics-ML) are directly relevant to hyperlocal weather emulation and super-resolution; RAPIDS could accelerate the sensor-fusion and historical-pattern pipeline; TensorRT could serve the trained forecast models. Current compute undisclosed — university/grant-scale, likely CPU + modest GPU.

## Interesting Trends

1. **The strongest early-stage NVIDIA fits in SEA are physical- and perception-AI, not fintech** — Despite three FSI names in today's batch, the highest-conviction GPU-native companies are the vision and physics-ML plays: WEHLO's hyperlocal flood/landslide forecasting (an Earth-2/FourCastNet-shaped workload), Jejakin's satellite carbon segmentation, EyeQ Tech's six-vertical edge CV, and Videotto's video-understanding pipeline. Each has an inference footprint that grows with deployment, versus the FSI SaaS names (CeePay, Eazy Digital) whose compute stays light until they add document AI or fraud analytics. NVIDIA's Inception leverage is highest where the workload is inherently GPU-bound.

2. **Accelerator credits are becoming the default early GPU on-ramp — and a competitive battleground** — Both AppSecAI and AssetFindr came through Antler cohorts bundling cloud/AI compute credits ($650K+ in AppSecAI's case), and Videotto/Jejakin are East Ventures–backed. At pre-seed the silicon vendor is almost always "undisclosed" because it's whatever the accelerator's cloud-credit partner provides. This is precisely the moment Inception membership matters: the vendor whose ecosystem (Morpheus for AppSecAI, RAPIDS + Metropolis for AssetFindr) the founder learns first tends to become the production default.

3. **SEA's local-language and sovereign-AI theme keeps surfacing as a GPU-serving story** — Botika's Bahasa Indonesia voice/NLP stack and WEHLO's barangay-level "Vertical AI" are both bets on serving under-served local contexts that global vendors ignore — and both resolve to in-region, low-latency inference (Riva/NeMo for Botika; Earth-2 for WEHLO). Consolidation is the counter-current: Igloo's acquisition of Eazy Digital shows SEA insurtech pooling data toward platform scale, which is where the downstream FSI-AI (underwriting, fraud, document AI) workloads will eventually concentrate.
