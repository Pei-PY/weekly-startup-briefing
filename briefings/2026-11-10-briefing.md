## Interesting Trends

1. **Vietnam's AI story is moving from funding to deployment** — Easy AI's agent partnership, VEEP's Korean programme win and the Vietnam–Japan AI research pact (November 10) all point to adoption and cross-border programmes rather than venture rounds as the news driver.
2. **Malaysia's climate hardware is capital-starved** — EcoNiLi's RM8M total against RM50M+ of plant investment shows recyclers funding operations through corporate and state-linked capital, leaving room for software-led efficiency gains.
3. **Singapore seed valuations are holding up for revenue-first companies** — Multipolitan's $25M pre-money on $4M+ net revenue and Evo's preference-share raise after a brand divestiture show investors rewarding cash generation over growth narratives.
