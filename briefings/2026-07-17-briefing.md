### 1. Gene Solutions

**Vertical:** Healthcare & Life Sciences | **Total Raised to Date:** ~$36M | **Stage:** Pre-IPO (raising ~$50M) | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**What we do:** Gene Solutions is a Vietnam- and Singapore-headquartered precision-medicine company running NGS-based genetic testing — cell-free DNA NIPT, ctDNA cancer screening, genomic profiling, and treatment monitoring — with AI-enhanced sequence analysis. It operates nine NGS laboratories (two CAP-accredited central labs in Singapore and Vietnam), has processed 3M+ AI-enhanced NGS tests, and serves 4,500+ hospitals and clinics across 13+ markets. Revenue grew from $15M (2021) to $40M (2023), a 63% CAGR.

**Today's interesting [article](https://www.tradingview.com/news/reuters.com,2025:newsml_L4N3WU0IA:0-vietnam-s-gene-solutions-seeks-100-million-from-pre-ipo-and-2026-listing/):** Gene Solutions is seeking ~$50M from a pre-IPO round ahead of a targeted 2026 listing — making it the most fundable biotech in SEA and the region's clearest GPU-native genomics-at-scale story. With 3M+ NGS tests already processed and Element Biosciences / Complete Genomics-class sequencing partnerships in place, the secondary-analysis compute bill (alignment, variant calling) is a large, accelerable line item that today largely runs on CPU — exactly the gap NVIDIA Parabricks closes.

**Investors:** Mekong Capital (lead), plus strategic partners Element Biosciences and Raffles Medical Group (commercial collaborations)

**NVIDIA Relevancy:** Genomics secondary analysis (BWA-MEM alignment, GATK, DeepVariant) maps directly to Parabricks; multi-omics foundation-model work and variant-effect prediction align with BioNeMo; AI over pathology/imaging in oncology maps to MONAI; clinical-report generation and RAG over test results could use NeMo and NIM. ### 3. pQCee

**Vertical:** Cybersecurity | **Total Raised to Date:** ~$6.7M | **Stage:** Seed | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

This is the region's clearest post-quantum-security pure-play arriving exactly as NIST PQC migration deadlines drive enterprise urgency — and it maps unusually cleanly onto NVIDIA's cuPQC and CUDA-Q software stack for accelerated PQC and quantum-threat modeling.

**Investors:** SGInnovate, Lotus One Investment (co-leads), In Group Holdings, Wavemaker Ventures, SUTD Venture Holdings, Apsara Investments

## Interesting Trends

1. **Deep-tech from university spinouts is anchoring SEA's most NVIDIA-native deals** — Today's headline raise, pQCee (a SUTD spinout), maps almost one-to-one onto NVIDIA's newest software (cuPQC, cuQuantum, CUDA-Q), and Fabrica AI's construction-robot autonomy is a textbook Isaac ROS/Perceptor workload. The pattern from the June window (Synvo, Pints) holds: SEA's research-lab-to-startup pipeline is the region's most reliable source of GPU-native early-stage companies, and post-quantum security is now a distinct, fundable category in Singapore.

2. **Vertical AI in SEA is compute-light on the surface, compute-heavy underneath** — Six of today's ten (Gene Solutions genomics, Ricult satellite imagery, Prep speech AI, Flagright AML, plus Ringkas/First Circle credit scoring) present as "software" companies but sit on top of genuinely accelerable workloads — NGS secondary analysis, multispectral image processing, real-time ASR, streaming AML, and GPU-XGBoost credit models. The recurring gap is the same: high-value pipelines still running on CPU, exactly where Parabricks, cuCIM, Riva, Morpheus, and RAPIDS convert cost into speed.

3. **Credit and compliance are SEA's densest NVIDIA-adjacent cluster** — Four of ten (Flagright, Ringkas, First Circle, plus Ricult's bank JV) are running credit scoring, AML, or fraud screening that map to the same bunq/RAPIDS + Morpheus reference stack. As MAS, OJK, and BSP tighten AML and responsible-lending expectations through H2 2026, the explainability and false-positive-reduction metrics (Flagright's up-to-93% cut) become sales urgency — and the natural entry point for NVIDIA FSI engagement across the region's lenders and regtechs.
