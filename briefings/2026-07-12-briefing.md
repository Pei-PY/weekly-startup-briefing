### 1. WIZ.AI

**Vertical:** Telecom | **Total Raised to Date:** ~$56M | **Stage:** Series B | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**What we do:** WIZ.AI builds enterprise conversational voice agents ("Talkbots") that hold natural, human-sounding phone and omnichannel conversations in local Southeast Asian languages and dialects — Bahasa Indonesia, Thai, Vietnamese, Singlish, and more — for banks, telcos, and insurers. Founded 2019, Singapore HQ. It was among the first to deploy enterprise GenAI/LLM customer-engagement solutions in the region, positioning around localisation accuracy in complex low-resource languages.

**Today's interesting [article](https://www.prnewswire.com/apac/news-releases/voice-agent-pioneer-wizai-raises-tens-of-millions-in-series-b-to-scale-enterprise-agi-solution-globally-302604934.html):** Sunday telecom-AI read: WIZ.AI's Series B (tens of millions, led by SMBC Asia Rising Fund with Kasikorn Bank's Beacon VC) is one of the clearest signals that SEA's regulated enterprises are buying localized voice AI as production infrastructure, not pilots. **Investors:** SMBC Asia Rising Fund (lead), Beacon Venture Capital (Kasikorn Bank), SMIC SG Holdings, GGV Capital, Wavemaker Partners (earlier rounds)

## Interesting Trends

1. **Sensory and signal AI is SEA's most differentiated deep-tech wedge** — MUI Robotics (smell/taste foundation model) and Groundup.ai (acoustic-first predictive maintenance) are both building from-scratch neural models over non-visual sensor data, a genuinely novel modality with no incumbent to license from. These are the clearest examples in this issue of GPU-native training workloads where NVIDIA Inception engagement at seed/Series A stage is highest-leverage — before a competing accelerator ecosystem locks in.

2. **"Agentic" is moving from marketing to payment rails and workflow infrastructure** — Tazapay is explicitly building compliant rails for autonomous, AI-driven payments, while MindPal is the orchestration layer letting non-engineers ship multi-agent workflows. As agents begin to transact and execute business processes, the guardrails, fraud-scoring, and on-prem model-serving layers underneath (Morpheus, NeMo Guardrails, NIM) become the NVIDIA-relevant surface — even when the front-end is software-first.

3. **Geospatial and physical-world AI ties SEA startups to sovereign and industrial demand** — Thinking Machines (national satellite mapping), Delos (aquaculture yield/disease prediction), and Meey Group (digital mapping + 3D property twins) all convert imagery and sensor data about the physical world into decisions, at national or industry scale. These are the SEA workloads that most naturally pull in TensorRT/RAPIDS/cuCIM for imagery and Omniverse for 3D — and, for the public-sector cases, in-country sovereign-AI deployment.
