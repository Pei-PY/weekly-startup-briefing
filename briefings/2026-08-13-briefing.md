### 1. WISE AI

**Vertical:** FinTech / FSI | **Total Raised to Date:** Undisclosed (~$10M+ Series A) | **Stage:** Series A | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**What we do:** WISE AI is a Malaysian identity-verification company whose eKYC platform authenticates government-issued IDs, matches them against facial biometrics, and detects deepfakes for banking, insurance, fintech, telco, e-commerce and government customers. Kuala Lumpur HQ. It is the first and only Malaysian company certified by the U.S. NIST and to ISO 30107 (presentation-attack detection), and ranks in NIST's global top 25 for facial-recognition matching speed.

**Today's interesting [article](https://www.digitalnewsasia.com/startups/malaysian-startup-wise-ai-raises-8-figure-series-mtdc-vt-sbi-and-sunway-group):** WISE AI closed an eight-figure Series A from MTDC, VT-SBI and Sunway iLabs Ventures to scale eKYC across Southeast Asia. What stands out for Inception in August 2026: deepfake-driven identity fraud is the fastest-rising attack vector in APAC financial onboarding, and WISE AI runs its own facial-recognition SLMs and AI agents rather than reselling a foreign API — a rare deep-tech, GPU-native computer-vision moat in the SEA identity space, validated by an independent NIST top-25 ranking.

**Investors:** Malaysia Technology Development Corporation (MTDC), VentureTech SBI (VT-SBI), Sunway iLabs Ventures, PH Capital

**NVIDIA Relevancy:** Facial-recognition matching, liveness, and presentation-attack/deepfake detection are GPU-native computer-vision workloads: model training maps to TAO Toolkit and DALI; low-latency biometric inference to TensorRT and Triton; document/ID OCR to NeMo Retriever and document-AI NIMs; and fraud/anomaly signals across the KYC funnel to Morpheus. A NIST top-25 matching-speed result implies heavily optimized inference where TensorRT is the standard acceleration path.

**NVIDIA Case Study:** **bunq** — the European neobank ran AI-driven fraud and identity-risk detection with RAPIDS and GPU-accelerated pipelines, training models ~100× faster with a 5× faster data pipeline for transaction monitoring and onboarding integrity — the same fraud-and-identity surface WISE AI protects at KYC onboarding ([case study](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/bunq-financial-services-generative-ai/) · via [NVIDIA financial services page](https://www.nvidia.com/en-sg/industries/finance/))

### 2. Deep GI

**Vertical:** Healthcare & Life Sciences | **Total Raised to Date:** Undisclosed | **Stage:** Early / Pre-Seed (BOI pilot, spinout in formation) | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**Today's interesting [article](https://www.chula.ac.th/en/highlight/273772/):** Mid-August healthcare-AI signal: Deep GI is the strongest example this week of a SEA academic-medical spinout crossing from research to regulated product. Real-time endoscopy inference is intrinsically GPU-bound, making this a naturally NVIDIA-aligned clinical-edge candidate.

**Investors:** Not disclosed (Chulalongkorn University; Thailand BOI pilot support)

### 3. AThenaAI

**Vertical:** Healthcare & Life Sciences | **Total Raised to Date:** Undisclosed | **Stage:** Seed (angel) | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**What we do:** AThenaAI is a Thai genomics company that turns raw DNA-sequencing data into clinical-grade, easy-to-read health reports using AI, paired with at-home test kits and an app for results, self-care guidance and doctor teleconsults. It runs next-generation sequencing (NGS) and whole-exome sequencing (WES) pipelines and layers automated interpretation on top to summarize disease-risk and pharmacogenomic insights for consumers and clinicians.

**Today's interesting [article](https://athenaai.co/en/about/):** Mid-August genomics angle: consumer genomics in SEA has historically bottlenecked on interpretation, not sequencing — turning FASTQ/BAM files into a report a doctor and patient can act on is slow and expensive. AThenaAI's wedge is AI-automated interpretation of NGS/WES output, the single most GPU-accelerable step in the genomics stack. As Thailand builds out precision-medicine capacity in H2 2026, an interpretation-layer startup is exactly where accelerated bioinformatics (Parabricks/BioNeMo) delivers the sharpest cost-and-turnaround advantage.

**Investors:** Not disclosed (angel-backed)

NGS/WES workloads run on CPU by default today, which is precisely where Parabricks delivers up to ~100× speedups.
