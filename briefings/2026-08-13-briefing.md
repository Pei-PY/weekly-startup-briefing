### 1. SynaXG

**Vertical:** Telecom | **Total Raised to Date:** ~$20M | **Stage:** Seed | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**What we do:** SynaXG builds AI-native radio access network (AI-RAN) infrastructure for 5G and 6G telcos, embedding machine learning into the RAN so operators can run network functions and AI inference on the same accelerated compute. Singapore HQ, founded 2023. Its December 2025 seed of over US$20M — led by January Capital, Vertex Ventures and Qualgro — is one of Asia's largest early-stage AI-RAN bets, funding product development and telco-operator partnerships across North America, Europe and APAC.

**Today's interesting [article](https://technode.global/2025/12/03/singapores-synaxg-raises-over-20m-funding-to-accelerate-global-ai-ran/):** Mid-August rotate-in update: AI-RAN went from research curiosity to strategic priority in H2 2026, and SynaXG is the SEA pure-play positioned for it. NVIDIA's US$1B stake in Nokia (October 2025) and the SoftBank AITRAS field trials established AI-RAN as a category where every dollar of RAN infrastructure could return an estimated $5 in AI-inference revenue — the exact thesis SynaXG is selling to telcos. As the only Southeast Asian AI-RAN specialist at scale, it is the region's most strategically aligned Inception telecom candidate heading into the 6G standards cycle.

**Investors:** January Capital, Vertex Ventures SEA & India, Qualgro

**NVIDIA Relevancy:** AI-RAN is one of NVIDIA's most directly aligned software categories: SynaXG's L1/L2 acceleration and inline inference map to Aerial CUDA-Accelerated RAN and the Aerial AI-RAN framework; multi-tenant inference sharing on the RAN maps to Triton/Dynamo and NIM; network-AI security to Morpheus; and DPU-offloaded fronthaul processing to DOCA. **NVIDIA Case Study:** **SoftBank** — built "AITRAS," the world's first 5G AI-RAN field trial on NVIDIA-accelerated computing with the Aerial platform, running vRAN and AI inference concurrently on shared infrastructure to unlock idle RAN capacity for inference revenue — the exact converged AI-RAN architecture SynaXG productizes ([case study](https://www.telecomstechnews.com/news/softbank-and-nvidia-collaborate-ai-ran-technology/) · via [NVIDIA telecommunications page](https://www.nvidia.com/en-sg/industries/telecommunications/))

### 2. WISE AI

**Vertical:** FinTech / FSI | **Total Raised to Date:** Undisclosed (~$10M+ Series A) | **Stage:** Series A | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**What we do:** WISE AI is a Malaysian identity-verification company whose eKYC platform authenticates government-issued IDs, matches them against facial biometrics, and detects deepfakes for banking, insurance, fintech, telco, e-commerce and government customers. Kuala Lumpur HQ. It is the first and only Malaysian company certified by the U.S. NIST and to ISO 30107 (presentation-attack detection), and ranks in NIST's global top 25 for facial-recognition matching speed.

**Today's interesting [article](https://www.digitalnewsasia.com/startups/malaysian-startup-wise-ai-raises-8-figure-series-mtdc-vt-sbi-and-sunway-group):** WISE AI closed an eight-figure Series A from MTDC, VT-SBI and Sunway iLabs Ventures to scale eKYC across Southeast Asia. **Investors:** Malaysia Technology Development Corporation (MTDC), VentureTech SBI (VT-SBI), Sunway iLabs Ventures, PH Capital
