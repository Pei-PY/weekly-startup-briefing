**Vertical:** Auto / AV Infra | **Total Raised to Date:** ~$20.8M | **Stage:** Series A | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**What we do:** MooVita builds full-stack driverless software and integration kits — MooBus (autonomous shuttle) and MooBox (a retrofit self-driving kit) — that turn conventional vehicles into Level-4 autonomous platforms for first/last-mile, campus, logistics, and utility transport. **Vertical:** Robotics | **Total Raised to Date:** ~$400K | **Stage:** Seed | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**What we do:** Drift builds an AI co-pilot for robotics simulation — a natural-language agent that helps engineers set up, debug, and run simulations across ROS, Gazebo, and the broader sim stack, cutting robot-deployment cycles from months to days. Founded July 2025, incubated at Antler Singapore (Disrupt 2025 cohort).
