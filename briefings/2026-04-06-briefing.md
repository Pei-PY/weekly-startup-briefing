### 1. Nomadic (NomadicML)

**Vertical:** Auto / AV Infra | **Total Raised to Date:** $8.4M | **Stage:** Seed | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**What we do:** Nomadic converts unstructured AV fleet footage and sensor data into structured, semantically searchable datasets using vision-language models. Engineering teams at AV companies can search petabytes of footage by describing what they want to find — "pedestrian crossing at night in rain" — rather than manually scrubbing video.

**Today's interesting [article](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/31/nomadic-raises-8-4-million-to-wrangle-the-data-pouring-off-avs/):** Nomadic won first prize at NVIDIA GTC's startup pitch competition in March 2026 and counts Zoox and Mitsubishi Electric as customers at seed stage — unusually strong enterprise validation for a sub-$10M company. Jeff Dean (Google DeepMind) and angels from OpenAI and Google DeepMind invested personally.

**Investors:** TQ Ventures (lead), Pear VC, Jeff Dean (Google DeepMind), angels from OpenAI and Google DeepMind

**NVIDIA Relevancy:** Nomadic's VLM-based video indexing pipeline is GPU-intensive and could potentially run on H100 or Jetson at the fleet edge. TensorRT-LLM and Triton Inference Server may be relevant to their inference serving stack.

## Interesting Trends

*Three macro signals emerging from today's news:*

1. **The genuine origination opportunity is concentrated at seed and Series A** — After applying the $200M cap, Salesforce opportunity filters, and active-user exclusions, every company in the final 10 except Linx Security ($83M) and Manna ($110M) has raised under $50M. The pattern is clear: by the time a startup reaches Series C or $200M+ raised, NVIDIA has typically already engaged them through enterprise sales or NVentures. Nomadic ($8.4M, GTC pitch winner, Zoox and Mitsubishi as customers) and Soma Energy ($7M, 5 paying AI data center customers before institutional capital) are the archetype of what this briefing should surface — companies with verified enterprise traction but no Salesforce presence.

2. **Physical AI data infrastructure is emerging as a fundable category in its own right** — Nomadic (AV data structuring via VLM, GTC winner) and Whirl AI (IT knowledge graph + AI agents, ex-NVIDIA VP founder) both represent the same thesis: the physical and enterprise worlds generate unstructured data faster than software can process it, and a new layer of GPU-accelerated data infrastructure is required to make physical and enterprise AI tractable. Both are at seed stage, not yet in Salesforce as active accounts, and building infrastructure that naturally runs on NVIDIA's stack.

3. **Agentic enterprise AI is converging on compliance-heavy expert workflows as its first durable wedge** — Numos (CFO financial AI, General Catalyst), Daydream (GEO agent fleet, First Round + WndrCo), and Whirl AI (IT transformation agents, ICONIQ first seed) all raised this week on a shared thesis: the highest-value agentic AI applications are in narrow, compliance-sensitive workflows where auditability matters more than breadth, and where public cloud AI is not viable. These companies will drive on-premise NIM deployments and RAPIDS usage in production — and represent the next generation of NVIDIA AI Enterprise customers.
