## Interesting Trends

1. **Malaysia is entering the front-end semiconductor design era** — GreatAsic's Pre-Series A marks a genuine inflection: Malaysia's semiconductor industry has historically been backend (assembly, packaging, test) but GreatAsic, SkyeChip, and Oppstar are now designing AI chips from scratch. With Arm IP access secured and Vertex Ventures backing, Malaysia has the ingredients to build a chip design cluster alongside its existing manufacturing base — a development NVIDIA should track closely as it expands its Inception footprint in the country.

2. **SuperAI Singapore 2026 signals AI Week deal flow** — Today's SuperAI opening (10,000 attendees, 1,500 AI companies) historically precedes a burst of Singapore-anchored deal announcements in the following 2–4 weeks. Watch deal flow from Singapore-based AI infrastructure, fintech AI, and enterprise AI startups through June 21 as event conversations convert to signed term sheets.

3. **Physical AI and software AI are developing asymmetrically in SEA** — GreatAsic (custom silicon), Polybee (autonomous drones), and Muun AI (factory telemetry) are all physical AI plays raising at pre-seed or seed stage for technology that has clear immediate ROI. In contrast, software AI companies (Level3AI, Sapiens AI) are raising at 3–20× higher valuations per dollar of revenue. The valuation gap between physical and software AI in SEA is a structural inefficiency — and NVIDIA's Jetson ecosystem is uniquely positioned to accelerate physical AI companies that current VCs are undervaluing.
