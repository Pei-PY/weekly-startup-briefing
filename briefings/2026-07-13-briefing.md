### 1. ReN3

**Vertical:** AI Developer Tools | **Total Raised to Date:** $5M | **Stage:** Seed | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**What we do:** ReN3 is a Singapore enterprise **agentic AI work platform** built for regulated industries — a no-code agent builder that runs across air-gapped on-premise systems, private cloud, and hybrid, and can process 80+ file formats. Teams assemble AI agents for administration, legal, finance, procurement, and HR without shipping data to a public API. Customers already include HTX, SBS Transit, SM Prime, Monde Nissin, and NAT Re; go-to-market runs through Dell Technologies, Red Hat, and NTT DATA Philippines.

**Today's interesting [article](https://technode.global/2026/05/05/singapores-ai-work-platform-firm-ren3-raises-5m-seed-round-led-by-vertex-ventures/):** ReN3 raised a $5M seed led by Vertex Ventures SEA & India (5 May 2026) to scale enterprise agentic AI across Southeast Asia, with a workflow-builder beta due Q3 2026 and a public agent marketplace before year-end. The Inception-relevant angle: ReN3's entire wedge is **sovereign, on-prem agentic AI** — exactly the deployment posture that makes NVIDIA's on-prem inference stack (NIM, NeMo, air-gapped NVAIE) a natural fit, and its Dell/Red Hat channel means it sells into precisely the hardware footprints NVIDIA wants agents running on.

**Investors:** Vertex Ventures SEA & India (lead); channel/tech partners: Dell Technologies, Red Hat, NTT DATA Philippines

**NVIDIA Relevancy:** ReN3's air-gapped agent runtime is a textbook **NIM on-prem** deployment; agent orchestration maps to **NeMo Agent Toolkit** and **NeMo Guardrails**; its 80+ file-format ingestion maps to **NV-Ingest** and **NeMo Retriever**; and low-latency serving in regulated data centres maps to **TensorRT-LLM** and **Triton/Dynamo**. Because ReN3 already co-sells with Dell and Red Hat, an NVIDIA reference architecture (NVAIE + NIM on certified on-prem systems) could potentially become the default substrate for its regulated-industry deployments.

**NVIDIA Case Study:** **ServiceNow** — co-develops native enterprise AI agents on NeMo and NIM (incl. the Apriel Nemotron model), embedding agentic AI directly into its platform — the closest NVIDIA reference for a no-code enterprise agent builder like ReN3 ([case study](https://www.servicenow.com/company/media/press-room/nvidia-agentic-ai-partnership.html) · via site search — AI Developer Tools maps by customer industry served)

### 2. Neptune Robotics

**Vertical:** Robotics | **Total Raised to Date:** ~$60M (incl. $52M Series B) | **Stage:** Series B | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**What we do:** Neptune Robotics builds **autonomous underwater ROVs that clean ship hulls** — removing biofouling to cut drag, fuel burn, and emissions for the shipping industry. Founded in Hong Kong in 2019, it relocated its HQ to Singapore in 2025. Its robots operate across 61 ports spanning Singapore and China and service ~70% of Asia's primary shipping lanes. A new US$12M Singapore factory (announced April 2026, on top of a US$52M Series B led by Granite Asia with NYK Line) targets a 400% capacity increase toward 60 daily hull cleanings by 2027.

**Today's interesting [article](https://technode.global/2026/04/20/neptune-robotics-invests-12m-in-new-singapore-factory-to-boost-autonomous-ship-hull-cleanings/):** Neptune is pouring US$12M into a Singapore robotics factory to five-fold its autonomous cleaning capacity — a rare hardware-heavy physical-AI scale-up in SEA. The maritime-decarbonization thesis is concrete: fouled hulls can raise fuel consumption double digits, so every autonomous cleaning is a measurable emissions cut. NYK Line (one of the world's largest shipping companies) on the cap table is a powerful commercial signal that robotic hull cleaning is becoming standard maritime practice.

**Investors:** Granite Asia (Series B lead), NYK Line (strategic maritime partner), existing shareholders

**NVIDIA Relevancy:** Autonomous underwater navigation and biofouling detection are edge-vision + sensor-fusion workloads that map to **Isaac ROS** (cuVSLAM, nvblox for 3D reconstruction), **JetPack**, and **DeepStream** for underwater video analytics, with **TensorRT** for on-ROV defect/fouling inference. Fleet scheduling across 61 ports could potentially use **cuOpt**, and pre-deployment testing of new hull geometries maps to **Isaac Sim** digital twins. Neptune is a flagship SEA physical-AI account with recurring per-cleaning economics rather than one-off hardware sales.

**NVIDIA Case Study:** **Saildrone** — its autonomous ocean vehicles process sensor streams on NVIDIA Jetson at the edge and are optimizing perception with DeepStream — the closest NVIDIA maritime-autonomy reference for Neptune's underwater hull-cleaning ROVs ([case study](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/saildrone-autonomous-oceanic-monitoring-jetson-deepstream/) · via [NVIDIA robotics page](https://www.nvidia.com/en-sg/industries/robotics/))

### 3. NamiTech

**Vertical:** Cybersecurity | **Total Raised to Date:** $6M | **Stage:** Pre-Series A | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**What we do:** NamiTech (Nami Technology) is a Ho Chi Minh City **voice-AI and voice-biometrics** company spun off from Vietnam's FPT Corporation in 2022. Its stack: **VoiceDNA** (voice biometric authentication — claimed 95% accuracy, 10-second voiceprint enrolment, anti-spoofing, text-dependent and text-independent verification), **NamiSense** (conversation analytics), and **CrystalSound** (noise suppression / audio enhancement). It sells into AI-driven CRM and secure authentication across APAC.

**Today's interesting [article](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202601/vietnams-namitech-secures-4m-to-expand-voice-biometrics-across-apac):** NamiTech raised $4M (January 2026) led by Japanese energy group Toho Gas with Thien Viet Securities returning, bringing total funding to $6M. Voice biometrics is having a moment as deepfake voice fraud surges — VoiceDNA's anti-spoofing and liveness verification put NamiTech squarely in the fraud-defense category. As an FPT spinout, it has enterprise distribution most seed-stage voice startups can only dream of.

**Investors:** Toho Gas (lead), Thien Viet Securities (TVS), FPT Corporation (incubator/parent)

**NVIDIA Relevancy:** Voice biometrics is a GPU-native speech-AI workload: speaker verification maps to **NeMo TitaNet** speaker-embedding models and **Riva** speaker recognition; real-time noise suppression (CrystalSound) maps to the **Maxine Audio Effects SDK**; low-latency enrolment/verification maps to **TensorRT**; and anti-spoofing/deepfake detection is a natural **Morpheus** + NeMo classifier pipeline. NamiTech is a strong candidate for on-prem **NIM** speech microservices where financial and government customers need voiceprints to stay in-country.

**NVIDIA Case Study:** **Amdocs** — built voice-capable customer-care agents on NeMo and NIM measuring 4–6× faster inference and ~80% lower latency — the closest NVIDIA reference for productionizing real-time voice AI like NamiTech's VoiceDNA/CrystalSound ([case study](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/case-studies/amdocs-builds-generative-ai-agents-for-telecom/) · via [NVIDIA telecommunications page](https://www.nvidia.com/en-sg/industries/telecommunications/))

## Interesting Trends

1. **Sovereign, on-prem agentic AI is becoming SEA's dominant enterprise wedge** — ReN3 (air-gapped agents for regulated industries) and OrtCloud (deterministic, isolated compute for agents) are two sides of the same bet: enterprises and governments want autonomous agents, but only if the compute and data stay sovereign and always-on. This is precisely NVIDIA's NIM/NVAIE/on-prem thesis, and SEA's data-residency regulations make it structural rather than cyclical.

2. **Physical AI in SEA is maturing from demos to recurring-revenue infrastructure** — Neptune Robotics is building a Singapore factory to 5× autonomous hull cleanings with NYK Line as a strategic backer, while Kitar turns phone grading into a computer-vision line. Both are recurring-usage physical-AI businesses (per-cleaning, per-inspection) rather than one-off hardware — exactly the profile where NVIDIA's edge stack (Jetson software, DeepStream, TensorRT) compounds.

3. **"AI-native" is being retrofitted onto SEA's SME and cleantech backbone** — SoBanHang (finance OS for 800K micro-merchants), Baskit (AI distribution + embedded credit), and NEU Battery Materials (electrochemical recycling meeting NVIDIA ALCHEMI) show the pattern: companies with real physical/financial distribution are adding a GPU-accelerated AI layer on top of proprietary data. The moat is the data and distribution; the AI is the margin expander.
