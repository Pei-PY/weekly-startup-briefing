### 1. Sharpa

**Vertical:** Robotics | **Total Raised to Date:** undisclosed | **Stage:** Seed (est.) | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**What we do:** Sharpa builds high-end dexterous robotic hands for humanoid and industrial manipulation. Its flagship **Sharpa Wave** is a five-finger hand with 22 degrees of freedom and a high-resolution Digital Tactile Array (>1,000 pixels per fingertip, 0.02N pressure sensitivity) that supports in-hand rotation and bimanual tasks. Singapore HQ.

**Today's interesting [article](https://careersinrobotics.com/guides/top-robotics-companies-singapore):** On June 1, 2026 in Singapore, NVIDIA and Sharpa unveiled the **Isaac GR00T Reference Humanoid Robot** — the first dexterous humanoid reference design on the GR00T platform to feature Sharpa's Wave hands, pairing a Unitree H2 Plus body (75 total DOF with the hands) and NVIDIA Jetson Thor onboard compute with Isaac GR00T software. The platform is slated for October 2026 release. For Inception, this is the rare SEA company that is not just NVIDIA-adjacent but a named hardware partner inside NVIDIA's own physical-AI reference stack.

**Investors:** Not disclosed

### 3. MFast

**Vertical:** FinTech / FSI | **Total Raised to Date:** ~$10M (Series A ~$6M) | **Stage:** Series A | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**What we do:** MFast (by DigiPay) is a Vietnamese agent-banking app that distributes loans and insurance to tier 2–3 provinces through a network of ~160,000 active agents who introduce, educate, and onboard rural customers. It prequalifies applicants using predictive ML that flags high-default risk, and matches insurance buyers to plans via a data-driven engine. Founded 2019; has distributed ~$217M in financial products and ~$2.2M in insurance.

**Today's interesting [article](https://www.innreg.com/blog/vietnam-fintech-trends):** MFast's model — human agents plus AI risk scoring — is the pragmatic answer to Vietnam's thin-file rural credit problem, where digital-only acquisition fails. **Investors:** Do Ventures, JAFCO Asia, Wavemaker Partners (among others)
