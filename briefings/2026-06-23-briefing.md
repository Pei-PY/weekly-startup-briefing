### 1. Ropedia

**Vertical:** Robotics | **Total Raised to Date:** ~$20M+ (seed, "tens of millions") | **Stage:** Seed | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**What we do:** Ropedia builds data infrastructure for Physical AI — capturing in-the-wild human experience at scale and transforming it into structured multimodal data that powers robotics, embodied AI, and world models. Its core product HOMIE is a wearable, lightweight capture system that collects first-person multimodal signals, which its proprietary 4D reconstruction and alignment algorithms convert into dynamic world representations more suitable for robot training than traditional video. Singapore HQ, founded late 2025.

**Today's interesting [article](https://pandaily.com/ropedia-raises-tens-of-millions-in-seed-funding-to-build-robotics-data-infrastructure):** Ropedia raised tens of millions in seed from North American angels associated with Google, NVIDIA, and Amazon plus Asia-based USD funds — an unusually deep bench for a company this young. The founding team is elite: CEO Dr. Chen Zhaoxi (ex-Meta optical motion capture), CTO Dr. Hong Fangzhou (ex-Meta first-person multimodal research), and Chief Scientist Prof. Liu Ziwei (NTU computer-vision lab). The pitch — cutting robot training-data cost ~50× — targets the single biggest bottleneck in the humanoid/embodied-AI race.

**Investors:** North American angels linked to Google, NVIDIA, and Amazon; Asia-based USD venture funds (names undisclosed)

**NVIDIA Relevancy:** This is the most GPU-native company in today's briefing. Ropedia's 4D reconstruction and world-model data pipelines could potentially map to Isaac GR00T-Dreams and Cosmos world-foundation-model blueprints for synthetic trajectory generation; Isaac Sim and Isaac Lab may align with simulation-based data augmentation; the reconstruction/alignment stack is relevant to nvblox, cuVSLAM, and DALI; and downstream robot-policy training maps to Megatron-Core, Transformer Engine, and NCCL. NVIDIA-linked angels on the cap table signal existing ecosystem proximity.

**NVIDIA Case Study:** **1X** — its humanoid robot autonomously performed domestic tidying tasks using a policy post-trained on NVIDIA Isaac GR00T N1, where combining synthetic and real data improved model performance 40% versus real data alone — the exact real-plus-synthetic data economics Ropedia's infrastructure is built to deliver ([case study](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-isaac-gr00t-n1-open-humanoid-robot-foundation-model-simulation-frameworks) · via [NVIDIA robotics page](https://www.nvidia.com/en-sg/industries/robotics/))

### 2. Meticuly

**Vertical:** Healthcare & Life Sciences | **Total Raised to Date:** $8.3M | **Stage:** Series B | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**What we do:** Meticuly designs and 3D-prints patient-specific titanium bone, cranial, and maxillofacial implants generated directly from a patient's CT scans using AI-assisted segmentation and modeling software. The pipeline turns raw imaging into a bespoke implant engineered to the individual's anatomy to improve surgical fit, outcomes, and OR efficiency. Bangkok, Thailand HQ, founded 2017; works with 850+ surgeons and 2,500+ patients across 240+ hospitals.

**Today's interesting [article](https://www.trysignalbase.com/news/funding/meticuly-secures-83m):** Meticuly closed an $8.3M Series B on June 23, 2026, to scale its patient-specific implant manufacturing. The company has already built a rare full-stack medical-device business out of Southeast Asia — regulatory-grade titanium implants produced from imaging in-house rather than outsourced — and the raise funds capacity and international expansion. A Thai deep-tech medtech reaching Series B on a hardware-plus-AI model is a standout in a region dominated by fintech and consumer apps.

**Investors:** Not disclosed in the funding announcement (Series B)

**NVIDIA Relevancy:** The imaging-to-implant pipeline is genuinely GPU-relevant. CT segmentation and 3D anatomical reconstruction could potentially map to MONAI and Clara medical-imaging models (MONAI segments 100+ anatomical structures from a single whole-body CT); real-time multi-modal imaging fusion for surgical planning may align with the Holoscan SDK; and generative implant-geometry and mesh workflows are relevant to Omniverse/OpenUSD and cuCIM for large-volume image processing. TensorRT could accelerate segmentation inference.

**NVIDIA Case Study:** **Medtronic / ImFusion** — built real-time surgical guidance that fuses multi-modal imaging using the NVIDIA Holoscan SDK for low-latency intraoperative inference — the closest NVIDIA reference for a medical-imaging-to-surgery pipeline like Meticuly's CT-to-implant workflow ([case study](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/real-time-surgical-guidance-by-fusing-multi-modal-imaging-with-nvidia-holoscan/) · via [NVIDIA healthcare & life sciences page](https://www.nvidia.com/en-sg/industries/healthcare-life-sciences/))

### 3. AI Hay

**Vertical:** Consumer AI / CRISP | **Total Raised to Date:** ~$18M | **Stage:** Series A | **NVIDIA Tech Alignment:** 🟢 Strong potential

**What we do:** AI Hay is a Vietnamese AI knowledge-discovery platform that blends generative AI with social community — users ask questions and get curated answers drawn from large language models, local content, and community experts. It is built for cultural and linguistic relevance to Vietnamese users rather than a generic chatbot wrapper. Vietnam HQ with a dedicated in-country engineering team.

**Today's interesting [article](https://technode.global/2025/07/02/vietnams-ai-hay-secures-10m-in-series-a-funding-round-led-by-argor-capital/):** AI Hay raised a $10M Series A led by Argor Capital, bringing total funding to over $18M. The traction is the headline: 15M+ downloads since a May 2024 launch, millions of daily active users, and 100M+ questions answered monthly — plus selection into AWS's Global Generative AI cohort as one of three SEA startups. That is consumer-scale LLM inference in a market where localized Vietnamese AI is still wide open.

**Investors:** Argor Capital (lead), Square Peg, Northstar Ventures, AppWorks, Phoenix Holdings

**NVIDIA Relevancy:** Consumer LLM inference at this volume is a high-throughput serving problem. TensorRT-LLM and NIM microservices could potentially cut cost-per-query and latency for a Vietnamese-language model; NeMo and NeMo Curator may align with fine-tuning and building a Vietnamese corpus; NeMo Retriever and cuVS are relevant to the retrieval layer over local content; and Riva could apply if voice input is added. Currently AWS-hosted (per its GenAI-cohort selection), so silicon vendor is cloud-abstracted.

**NVIDIA Case Study:** **Amdocs** — cut RAG token consumption up to 60% and achieved 4–6× faster inference with ~80% lower latency by serving domain LLMs on NVIDIA NIM — directly analogous to serving a localized consumer LLM economically across 100M+ monthly queries ([case study](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/amdocs-accelerates-generative-ai-performance-and-lowers-costs-with-nvidia-nim/) · via site search — Consumer AI / CRISP maps by LLM-serving use case)
