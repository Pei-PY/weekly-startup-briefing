A company is disqualified from the briefing if any of the following conditions are true: total capital raised to date exceeds $200M (the briefing targets early-stage companies before they accumulate NVIDIA attention); the company's Salesforce Account record carries an "Enterprise Customer" tag in AccountTags__c (signals an active NVIDIA enterprise sales relationship); the company's Inception Connect Profile shows a Membership_Status__c of "Member - High Touch" or "Member - Nominated High Touch" (already receiving dedicated Inception support); the company has 2 or more Opportunity records linked to its Salesforce Account (signals the NVIDIA field team is already actively covering them); the story's publication timestamp is older than 24 hours from the time of the research run (stale news); or the "company" is a VC fund announcement rather than an operating startup.
