A company is disqualified from the briefing if any of the following conditions are true: total capital raised to date exceeds $200M (the briefing targets early-stage companies before they attract broad attention); the company has already been featured in an earlier briefing (each company appears in the series once; follow-up news belongs in Interesting Trends); the story's publication timestamp is older than 24 hours from the time of the research run (stale news); or the "company" is a VC fund announcement rather than an operating startup.
